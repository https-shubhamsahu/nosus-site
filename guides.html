How do I send a one-time note or file without an account?

Burn Notes and Burn Files work without an account for either person. Create a note or select files on nosus.foo, choose an expiry, and share the one-time link. Burn Files accepts up to 10 files with a combined size of 50 MB.

  1. Open the Burn tool and choose Note or File.
  2. Write the note or select your files, choose an expiry, then create the share link.
  3. Send the complete link to the intended recipient. A used or expired link cannot be opened again.

Limits to know

One-time access does not prevent the recipient from saving, copying, printing or photographing the content. A single note or file also gets a two-digit code: while that code is valid (20 minutes by default), its key is held on the server too. The direct link carries its key in the URL fragment. Multi-file shares are link-only. Treat the complete link as sensitive; never paste it into an analytics or AI service.

How do I open my Drive on a borrowed computer?

Go opens the Saved items you approve on a borrowed computer without giving that computer your Google password or token. Your phone is the Drive client. Saved and Go are available in the app; the computer's approved session lasts at most 60 minutes.

  1. Set up Saved and your Drive connection in the NO SUS app on your own device.
  2. Open nosus.foo/go on the computer in front of you. Scan its QR with the phone app and confirm the two-digit match code.
  3. Approve with your phone's screen lock and select the items you want to open. Close the session when you finish.

Limits to know

Only approve a code on a screen physically in front of you. Downloads and prints can remain on the borrowed computer after the session ends. Session closure does not erase those copies. Go is not a promise that an untrusted computer cannot capture displayed content.

What do watermarks and revocation protect?

SecureSend adds viewer-entered email watermarks and an access history to shared documents. Links end after 30 days by default, and you can revoke one at any time to stop new opens. A watermark is an attribution clue, not proof of a verified identity.

  1. Open the NO SUS app and choose the document you want to share through SecureSend.
  2. Set the share's access limits and expiry, then send its link to the intended recipients.
  3. Review access history in the app. Revoke the share when you no longer want it open.

Limits to know

Revoking stops new opens; someone already viewing may keep the page they have open. Previously issued download URLs may remain valid briefly. Downloaded files, screenshots and prints cannot be recalled. Browser screenshot blocking is not possible, and SecureSend documents are not end-to-end encrypted.

How can someone send me files without signing up?

Drop gives a signed-in NO SUS user a personal address such as riya-notes.nosus.foo. Senders open the address in a browser without an account. You open the door when expecting a file, then preview and accept it before it reaches your Drive.

  1. Choose your handle in the Android or web app and share your personal address or QR code.
  2. Open your Drop door when you are expecting files. Ask the sender to open your address and select the files in their browser.
  3. Preview the incoming file and accept it into Drive only when you recognize and want it.

Limits to know

The owner needs an account and a configured Drive connection; the sender does not. Existing nosus.foo/to?h=<handle> links still work. A personal Drop is different from a group drop: group copies land in each member's Drive, and leaked group-file watermark attribution is not available yet.
