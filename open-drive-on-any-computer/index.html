Open your files on a borrowed PC without typing your password

Open your NO SUS domain, like riya.nosus.foo, on the borrowed computer. Scan its QR with the NO SUS app, match the number and approve with your screen lock. Your NO SUS Chat opens there for 15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours. The computer never receives your Google password or token, and your files stay in your own Google Drive.

Open the web app
Type your domain at nosus.foo/goAndroid beta

How it works in five steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Keep the file in your chat

    On your phone, add the file to NO SUS Chat. It is your own chat, stored in your own Google Drive.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Open your domain

    On the borrowed computer, open your domain in any browser. A QR code appears. Forgot it? Type your handle at nosus.foo/go.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Scan and match

    Scan the QR with the NO SUS app and tap the number that matches the one on the computer.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Approve

    Approve with your phone’s screen lock. Your chat opens on the computer.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Close when you’re done

    Close the tab when you finish, or tap End on your phone. Left unused, the session ends after 10 minutes (an hour on an 8-hour session), and it never lasts past 8 hours.

Honest limits

  • NO SUS Chat shows what is in your chat, not your whole Drive, so add the file to the chat first.
  • Downloads and prints may stay on that computer after the session ends.
  • The computer sees whatever you open on it, so only approve a number shown on the screen in front of you.

Questions

Is incognito mode safe on a public computer?

It helps: Chrome deletes incognito history and cookies when you close all incognito windows. But you still type your password on that machine, and downloaded files stay on it.

Does the borrowed computer get my Google password or token?

No. Your phone stays the only thing signed in to Google. The computer shows your chat for the session you approve: 15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours, your pick.

What is my domain?

Your own address on NO SUS, your handle followed by .nosus.foo, like riya.nosus.foo. It shows the QR you scan. If you forget it, type your handle at nosus.foo/go.

When does the session end?

When the time you picked runs out (15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours, and never past 8 hours even if you add time), after 10 minutes without use on that computer (an hour on an 8-hour session), or when you tap End on your phone or close the tab. Then the computer can no longer show your chat. Anything already downloaded or printed stays on that computer.

How do I sign in to Google on a public computer?

Google says to use a private window: in Chrome, open a Guest profile, sign in, and close the Guest window when you finish to delete history, cookies and site data. With NO SUS Chat you skip the sign-in and approve from your phone.

Do I need to install anything on the borrowed PC?

No. You only open your domain in its browser.
