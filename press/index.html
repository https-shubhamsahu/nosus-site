Press kit

Words, facts and images for writing about NO SUS.

Describe it

One line
NO SUS keeps your files on every screen and lets you choose how you send them.
50 words
NO SUS is an app with two tabs. Chat is your own chat, stored in your Google Drive, and it opens on any screen from your domain. Send has three ways to share: one open, watermarked or traceable copies. Self-destructing notes and files work on nosus.foo with no account.
100 words
NO SUS keeps your files on every screen and lets you choose how you send them. The app has two tabs. Chat is your own chat, stored in your Google Drive. Open your domain on a borrowed computer, scan its QR with the app and approve. That screen never gets your Google password. Send has three ways. One open: encrypted on your device, opened once, then deleted. Watermarked: each viewer's typed email is stamped across the document. Traceable copies: each reader gets a slightly reworded copy of a note, so leaked text shows whose copy it was. The native app is Android.

Key facts

Website
nosus.foo
The name
NO SUS (nosus.foo) is a privacy app made in India. It is not the slang phrase "no sus" and not Brazil's public health system, SUS.
Web app
app.nosus.foo, on any phone or computer, iPhone included.
Android
The only native app. Closed beta on Google Play.
Sign-in
Google, for new accounts. One-open notes and files on nosus.foo need no account.
One open
A note, or up to 10 files with 50 MB combined. Opens once, then it is deleted.
Watermarked
Links end after 30 days by default. Revoking one stops new opens.
Traceable copies
Up to 100 readers per note. Text only for now.
Chat on any screen
15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours, picked on your phone.
Programs
Member of NVIDIA Inception.

What NO SUS is not

  • It is not open source.
  • Watermarked links are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • A browser cannot block screenshots. Screenshot blocking works in the Android app.
  • A copy that was downloaded, printed or photographed cannot be recalled.
  • Viewer emails and reader names are typed, not verified. A traceable copy is a strong hint, not proof.
  • If you ask for a six-digit code, our server holds its key until up to 10 minutes after the code is used or expires.
  • There is no native iOS, Windows or macOS app.
