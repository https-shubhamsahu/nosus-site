Send a file without an account or login

On nosus.foo you can send a file with no login, sign-up or email address, and your recipient needs none either. Add up to 10 files, 50 MB combined, and you get one link that opens once. Files are encrypted in your browser first. It is account-free, not anonymous: like any site, it sees your IP address.

Tap File · Encrypted in your browser

0/50,000

While a 6-digit code you ask for is valid (10 min), and up to 10 min after, the key is also held on our server.

How it works in four steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Open nosus.foo

    Use any browser on a phone or computer; there is nothing to sign in to or install.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Add your files

    Tap File above and add up to 10 files, 50 MB combined.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Pick how long it waits

    Choose 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days; an unopened file is deleted after that.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Send the link

    Tap Share, or copy the link into WhatsApp, email or any chat. The recipient opens it with no login and the link then stops working.

Honest limits

  • No account does not mean anonymous: the server sees your IP address like any website, and a salted hash of it is kept only to rate-limit uploads and swept after 24 hours.
  • Nothing checks who is opening the link, so whoever has it first gets the file.
  • If you ask for a 6-digit code, the key is held on our server until up to 10 minutes after the code is used or expires.
  • The cap is 10 files and 50 MB combined, so it suits documents and photos, not large videos or folders.

Questions

Can I send a file without creating an account?

Yes. On nosus.foo you add the files and get a link; you never register or sign in, and the recipient does not either.

Is sending a file without login anonymous?

Not fully. We do not ask for a name or email and the link carries none, but like any website the server sees your IP address when you connect. For file uploads it keeps a salted hash of it only to rate-limit abuse, swept after 24 hours.

How do I share a file with someone who has no account?

Make the link above and send it by WhatsApp, email or any chat. They open it in a browser, save the files, and need no login or app.

Does WeTransfer need an account to send?

WeTransfer's free-plan page says you need a free account to send, while the person receiving never needs to sign up or log in. Its free plan allows 3 GB total and up to 10 transfers in a rolling 30 days.

How long does a temporary file link last?

Until it is opened once, or until the expiry you pick: 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days. A 6-digit code opens once and lasts up to 10 minutes.

What is the biggest file I can send with no account?

Here it is 50 MB combined across up to 10 files. For bigger files, a service with a larger limit such as WeTransfer's 3 GB free cap is the better fit.
