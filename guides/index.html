Chat tab

How do I open my chat on another screen?

NO SUS Chat is your own chat, stored in your Google Drive. Open your domain, such as riya.nosus.foo, on a laptop, a borrowed computer or a friend's phone. It shows a QR. Scan it with the NO SUS app, match the number and approve. That screen never gets your Google password or token.

  1. Sign in to the NO SUS app with Google and pick your handle. Your domain is your handle followed by .nosus.foo.
  2. On the other screen, open your domain in any browser. Forgot it? Type your handle at nosus.foo/go.
  3. Scan the QR with the NO SUS app, tap the number shown on that screen and approve with your phone's screen lock. Close the tab when you finish.

Limits to know

Only approve a number shown on a screen physically in front of you. Your phone stays the only device signed in to Google, and your files stay in your own Drive. Downloads and prints can remain on a borrowed computer after the session ends, and a computer you do not trust can still record what it displays.

How do I open a PDF in NO SUS?

On Android, NO SUS appears in the Open with list for PDFs. Pick it from your files app, Drive or a chat app and the PDF opens in the NO SUS viewer, with screenshots blocked. Share safely sends it on through the Send tab.

  1. Tap a PDF in any Android app and choose Open with.
  2. Pick NO SUS. Choose Always to make it your PDF app, or Just once.
  3. Read it in the viewer. To pass it on, tap Share safely and choose how it is sent.

Limits to know

Screenshot blocking works in the Android app only. Opening a PDF in NO SUS does not change the original file, which stays wherever it came from.

Send tab

How do I send a note or file that opens once?

Turn on One open, or use Burn on nosus.foo with no account for either person. The note or files are encrypted in your browser, open once and are then deleted from our server. Burn Files accepts up to 10 files with a combined size of 50 MB.

  1. Open the Burn tool and choose Note or File, or in the app pick Send, then One open.
  2. Write the note or select your files, choose an expiry, then create the share link.
  3. Send the complete link to the intended recipient. A used or expired link cannot be opened again.
  4. No way to send a link? Tap Use a code instead and read out the 6-digit code.

Limits to know

One-time access does not prevent the recipient from saving, copying, printing or photographing the content. The link carries its key in the URL fragment, the part after the #, which browsers do not send to our server. The optional six-digit code opens a note, a file or several files with no link, once, for up to 10 minutes. While it is valid, its key is held on the server too. Six digits are guessable, so wrong codes are limited per network, and a six-digit code stops working once 600 wrong guesses have been made across the service since it was made. Treat the complete link as sensitive; never paste it into an analytics or AI service.

What do watermarked links protect?

A watermarked link asks each viewer for their email, stamps it across your document and keeps an access history. Links end after 30 days by default, and you can revoke one at any time to stop new opens. A watermark is an attribution clue, not proof of a verified identity.

  1. In the NO SUS app, open Send and pick the document.
  2. Choose Anyone with a link, turn on Watermarked and create the link.
  3. Review who opened it in the app. Revoke the link when you no longer want it open.

Limits to know

Revoking stops new opens; someone already viewing may keep the page they have open. Previously issued download URLs may remain valid briefly. Downloaded files, screenshots and prints cannot be recalled. A browser cannot block screenshots, so the page is watermarked and logged instead, and watermarked documents are not end-to-end encrypted.

Send tab

How do traceable copies show whose copy leaked?

Turn on Traceable copies when you send a note. Behind one link, every reader gets a slightly reworded copy with an invisible marker, for up to 100 readers. If the text leaks, paste it, or pick a screenshot in the Android app, and NO SUS shows whose copy it matches.

  1. In the NO SUS app, open Send, write the note and turn on Traceable copies.
  2. Send the one link to everyone. Each reader types a name and opens their own copy, with no account or app.
  3. If the text turns up somewhere, open the note and check the leak.

Limits to know

Traceable copies work for text today. Heavy paraphrasing or mixing two copies can defeat the match or point at the wrong copy. A match names whose copy leaked, not who shared it: a strong hint, not proof. Reader names are typed, not verified.

Send tab

Can someone without the app send me a file?

Yes, with Burn. They open nosus.foo, choose File, add up to 10 files and send you the link. Neither of you needs an account, and the files open once. NO SUS no longer gives you a page where strangers upload into your Drive.

  1. Ask them to open nosus.foo in any browser.
  2. They choose File in the Burn tool, add the files and create the link.
  3. They send you the link. Open it once and save what you need.

Limits to know

The file passes through our server encrypted and is deleted after one open or at expiry. If they ask for a six-digit code, its key is on our server too until up to 10 minutes after the code is used or expires. Nothing checks who the sender really is.
