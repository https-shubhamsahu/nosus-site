NO SUS for teams

A small office can use NO SUS today to send client documents without leaving copies in chats and inboxes: a link that opens once, a PDF stamped with the email each viewer types, or a note sent as traceable copies. Nobody who opens one needs an account. The team workspace isn’t built yet. We want our first three pilot offices.

Which offices is it for?

Any desk that sends one person’s document to one person. Collecting from many people at once isn’t built yet.

CA and tax firms
Send a filed return or a notice as a watermarked PDF. Ask the client to send PAN or Form 16 as a one-open link they make on nosus.foo.
HR and staffing desks
Send an offer letter stamped with the email the candidate types.
Clinics and labs
Send one report to one patient, by a link that opens once.
School and college offices
Send a marksheet or a fee receipt to one parent, not the class group.
Law offices
Send a draft clause or a figure as a note that opens once.

Getting an ID copy from someone? They can mask their Aadhaar copy first, then send it to you by a one-time link.

What is NO SUS building for teams?

Three things a team needs that NO SUS doesn’t have yet. No office has signed up. We want the first three, and we’ll design these with them.

  • One shared workspace for a team.
  • One link that many clients can upload to.
  • An open log you can export.

After that: documents that carry their own rules. Who may open it, how many times, and what an AI agent may read.

What doesn’t NO SUS have?

  • No SOC 2, ISO 27001 or HIPAA certificate.
  • No data room, e-signature or client portal.
  • No promise that files are stored only in India.
  • Watermarked documents aren’t end-to-end encrypted. One-open notes and files are encrypted in your browser.
  • A watermark traces a leak. It can’t stop a photo of the screen.

Security says what each way of sending protects, and what it can’t.

Questions offices ask

Do clients need an account or the app?

No. Anyone can open a NO SUS link in a browser. For a watermarked document they type their email first. It isn’t checked, and it is stamped on every page.

Is it safe to send Aadhaar or PAN this way?

It is safer than a chat attachment. A one-open file is encrypted in your browser and deleted from our server after the first download. It can’t stop the other person from keeping the file they downloaded.

Does NO SUS make a firm DPDP compliant?

No. No tool does that on its own, and we won’t pretend ours does. India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Rules were notified in November 2025 and most duties apply from May 2027. What NO SUS changes for you is practical: fewer copies of a client’s ID sitting in chats and inboxes, and a record of who opened what you sent.

Is it safe to upload a client’s document to an AI chatbot?

Treat it like sending the document to another company, because that is what it is. Check the tool’s data settings first, and cover what it doesn’t need, such as ID numbers.

What does it cost?

Sending is free today. The team version will be paid, and we haven’t set its price.
