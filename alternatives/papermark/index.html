Papermark alternative for individuals

Papermark is an open-source document-sharing tool you can self-host, with a free plan of 50 documents and 50 links; its watermark needs the Data Rooms plan. NO SUS (nosus.foo) watermarked links give one person a viewer-email watermark, view log and revoke, free to use today. They aren't end-to-end encrypted.

Android beta

How it works in four steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Pick the document

    In the NO SUS app (Android, or app.nosus.foo in any browser), open Send and pick the PDF.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Turn on Watermarked

    Choose Anyone with a link, turn on Watermarked and create the link.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Send it

    The viewer types their email and reads it in a watermarked viewer, with no account and no download button.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Check the log, revoke

    See each view's email, device, time and viewing time, and revoke the link when you're done.

Honest limits

  • Watermarked links are not end-to-end encrypted, and the viewer's email is typed, not verified; Papermark can verify it on its Business plan.
  • Papermark is better on openness and reach: you can read and self-host the code, and it has data rooms, a custom domain, page-by-page analytics, an API and team seats. NO SUS has none of these and can't be self-hosted.
  • NO SUS tracks each view with email, device, time and viewing time, not page by page.
  • A browser can't block screenshots or a phone camera on either service; revoking stops new access, not copies already taken.

Questions

Is Papermark free?

Papermark has a free plan with 50 documents, 50 links, 1 team member and page-by-page analytics. Password, expiry and downloads are available on it, but the watermark needs the Data Rooms plan.

Can I self-host Papermark?

Yes. Papermark is open source under AGPLv3, and its README lists Node.js, a PostgreSQL database, S3 or Vercel Blob storage and an email provider as prerequisites. NO SUS can't be self-hosted.

Does Papermark verify viewer emails?

Papermark's help center lists email verification, which checks that the address is real, under its Business plan. Its free plan only asks viewers to enter an email. NO SUS also takes a typed email and doesn't verify it.

Does Papermark watermark documents?

Yes, but its help center lists the watermark under the Datarooms plan. NO SUS puts the viewer's typed email on every watermarked link, free to use today.

Which is better for a founder sharing a pitch deck?

Papermark, if you want page-by-page analytics, a custom domain or to own the code. NO SUS fits a one-off PDF where you want a viewer-email watermark, a view log and a revoke button without setting anything up.
