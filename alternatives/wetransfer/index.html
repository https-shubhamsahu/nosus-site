WeTransfer alternative for private files

WeTransfer is the better pick for big transfers: Free allows 3 GB and 10 transfers per 30 days, and Ultimate goes up to 1 TB. NO SUS (nosus.foo) Burn Files is for small sensitive files: up to 10 files, 50 MB combined, one link that opens once, encrypted in your browser, no accounts.

How it works in four steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Add your files

    Open the File tab above and add up to 10 files, 50 MB combined.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Pick the expiry

    Choose 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days; an unopened link is deleted after that.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Create and send

    Tap Create a share link, then Share. No link possible? Under More, Use a code instead gives a 6-digit code that opens one or several files with no link.

  4. 4

    Step 4: They open and save

    The recipient opens the link in any browser, saves the files, and the link stops working.

Honest limits

  • NO SUS is not for large files: 50 MB combined is enough for PDFs and photos, not for videos, raw footage or project folders. Use WeTransfer or similar for those.
  • One-time means the link opens once; whatever the recipient saves is a normal copy they can keep or forward.
  • The key travels in the link, but if you ask for a 6-digit code, our server holds the keys until up to 10 minutes after the code is used or expires.
  • WeTransfer has password protection and can limit a link to named emails; NO SUS has neither a password nor a recipient list.

Questions

What is a WeTransfer alternative for private files?

For small sensitive files, NO SUS Burn Files sends up to 10 files, 50 MB combined, as a link that works once. For anything larger, WeTransfer and similar services are the right tool, because NO SUS can't carry it.

How big a file can I send with WeTransfer?

WeTransfer's help center says Free allows 3 GB or 10 transfers in a rolling 30-day window, Starter 300 GB, and Ultimate up to 1 TB per transfer.

How long do WeTransfer links last?

On Free and Starter, links last up to 3 days. Ultimate lets you pick a custom expiry, up to never. A NO SUS link lasts until it is opened once, or 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days if unopened. A 6-digit code you ask for works once for up to 10 minutes.

Can I send a file with no account on NO SUS?

Yes. Neither the sender nor the recipient needs an account on nosus.foo, and it is free to use today. WeTransfer says recipients never need an account either.

Is NO SUS better than WeTransfer for a big video?

No. NO SUS caps a share at 50 MB combined, so it won't fit a video of any real length. Use WeTransfer for that, and NO SUS for the passport scan or contract that should open once.

Does WeTransfer have password protection?

Yes, on every plan including Free, according to WeTransfer's own pages. NO SUS uses a one-time link, or a 6-digit code you ask for, instead of a password.
