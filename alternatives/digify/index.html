Digify alternative for individuals

Digify is a document-security platform with DRM, data rooms and page-by-page analytics; its Pro plan is listed at $140 a month, and dynamic watermark is a $50 a month add-on. NO SUS (nosus.foo) watermarked links give one person a viewer-email watermark, view log and revoke, free to use today. They aren't end-to-end encrypted.

How it works in four steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Pick the document

    In the NO SUS app (Android, or app.nosus.foo in any browser), open Send and pick the PDF.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Turn on Watermarked

    Choose Anyone with a link, turn on Watermarked and create the link.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Send it

    The viewer types their email and reads it in a watermarked viewer, with no account and no download button.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Check the log, revoke

    See each view's email, device, time and viewing time, and revoke the link when you're done.

Honest limits

  • NO SUS has no DRM: once a viewer captures or photographs a page, nothing follows the copy. Digify's persistent protection after download goes further.
  • Watermarked links are not end-to-end encrypted, and the viewer's email is typed, not verified: an attribution clue, not proof.
  • Digify is far stronger for teams: data rooms, one-click NDAs, granular permissions, page-by-page analytics, integrations and SSO; NO SUS has none of these.
  • A browser can't block screenshots or a phone camera on either service; Digify describes Screen Shield as discouraging capture.

Questions

What is a free Digify alternative?

NO SUS watermarked links are free to use today and cover a viewer-email watermark, a view log and revoke for one PDF or image. Digify offers a free trial with no credit card, then paid plans.

How much does Digify cost?

Digify's comparison page lists the Pro plan at $140 a month. Its help center lists add-ons such as dynamic watermark and Screen Shield at $50 a month each, and extra users at $25 a month.

Does Digify watermark documents?

Yes. Digify lists dynamic, viewer-specific watermarks as a feature, and its help center sells Dynamic Watermark as a $50 a month add-on. Check which of its plans bundles it.

Can Digify or NO SUS stop screenshots?

Neither can guarantee it. Digify's Screen Shield limits document visibility and discourages screen capture. NO SUS turns off text selection and download and shows the viewer's email as a watermark, which deters but doesn't block.

Is NO SUS a replacement for Digify data rooms?

No. Digify has virtual data rooms, Q&A, audit logs and NDAs. NO SUS shares one document at a time through a watermarked link.
