Send a PDF as view once on WhatsApp

You can’t: WhatsApp view once only works for photos, videos and voice messages. Instead, make a Burn File here, encrypted in your browser, and paste its link into the chat. It opens once, then our server deletes it. Like any file, the person who opens it can still save or photograph it.

How it works in five steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Choose the PDF

    Tap File in the tool above and pick your PDF. Up to 10 files, 50 MB combined, fit in one link.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Pick an expiry

    If nobody opens it, the link expires after 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Create the link

    Your browser encrypts the file with AES-256 before upload and gives you a one-time link.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Paste it in WhatsApp

    Send the whole link: the key is in the part after the #.

  5. 5

    Step 5: It opens once

    After the first open the server deletes the file, and the link stops working.

Honest limits

  • Whoever opens it can save, forward or screenshot the PDF: one-time means the link, not their copy.
  • Anyone who has the full link can open it first, so send it only to the person you mean.
  • A browser can’t block screenshots, unlike view once photos inside WhatsApp.

Questions

Can you send a PDF as view once on WhatsApp?

No. View once is for photos, videos and voice messages. A PDF is sent as a normal document and stays in the chat.

Does the recipient need an app or account?

No. They tap the link and the file opens in their browser. Neither of you needs an account.

Can they screenshot or save it?

Yes. Once they open it, they can save the file or photograph the screen. One-time means the link stops working, not that their copy disappears.

What are the 2-digit and 6-digit codes?

A single file also gets a 2-digit code that works only with its link. Tap Use a code instead for a 6-digit code that opens one file or several with no link. While a code is valid (20 and 10 minutes by default) the key is also held on our server, then swept.

Is the file encrypted?

Yes. Your browser encrypts it with AES-256 before upload, and the direct link keeps the key in the part after the #.
