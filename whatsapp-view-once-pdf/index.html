Send a PDF as view once on WhatsApp

You can’t: WhatsApp view once only works for photos, videos and voice messages. Instead, make a Burn File here, encrypted in your browser, and paste its link into the chat. It opens once, then our server deletes it. Like any file, the person who opens it can still save or photograph it.

How it works in five steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Choose the PDF

    Tap File in the tool above and pick your PDF. Up to 10 files, 50 MB combined, fit in one link.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Pick an expiry

    If nobody opens it, the link expires after 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Create the link

    Your browser encrypts the file with AES-256 before upload and gives you a one-time link.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Paste it in WhatsApp

    Send the whole link: the key is in the part after the #.

  5. 5

    Step 5: It opens once

    After the first open the server deletes the file, and the link stops working.

How to send a view once PDF on WhatsApp from your phone

Open nosus.foo in your phone's browser, tap File and pick the PDF from your files or downloads. Create the link, tap Share and choose WhatsApp, then the chat. The PDF never goes into WhatsApp itself: only the link does.

Why WhatsApp has no view once option for documents

WhatsApp's view once pages list only photos, videos and voice messages, so a PDF is sent as an ordinary document that stays in the chat like any other message.

Can I send the PDF pages as view once photos instead?

For a one-page document, yes: turn the page into an image and send it with view once on. WhatsApp then blocks screenshots of it, though another phone's camera still works. For anything longer it gets clumsy: one photo per page, and the text can no longer be searched or copied.

View once on WhatsApp Web and Windows

WhatsApp's own how-to page says view once isn't available on the web app or on Windows: it is a phone feature. A one-time link has no such limit. Make it at nosus.foo on your computer, paste it into WhatsApp Web, and they can open it on a phone or a computer.

What your recipient sees

A link in the chat. Tapping it opens a page in their browser with a download button, so a chat preview of the link does not use it up. Downloading uses it: the file is deleted from our server, and the link in the chat stops working for everyone.

Their downloaded copy stays on their phone. For the screenshot rules inside WhatsApp, see can WhatsApp view once be screenshotted, and for other apps the view-once screenshot table.

Honest limits

  • Whoever opens it can save, forward or screenshot the PDF: one-time means the link, not their copy.
  • Anyone who has the full link can open it first, so send it only to the person you mean.
  • A browser can’t block screenshots, unlike view once photos inside WhatsApp.

Questions

Can you send a PDF as view once on WhatsApp?

No. View once is for photos, videos and voice messages. A PDF is sent as a normal document and stays in the chat.

Does the recipient need an app or account?

No. They tap the link and the file opens in their browser. Neither of you needs an account.

Can they screenshot or save it?

Yes. Once they open it, they can save the file or photograph the screen. One-time means the link stops working, not that their copy disappears.

What is the 6-digit code?

A way to send with no link. Under More, tap Use a code instead for a 6-digit code that opens one file or several. While it is valid, up to 10 minutes, the key is also held on our server, then deleted within 10 minutes after it is used or expires.

Can WhatsApp view once be screenshotted?

WhatsApp says it blocks screenshots of view once photos and videos. It can’t stop another phone from photographing the screen. A PDF is not view once media at all, so nothing blocks a screenshot of it.

How long does a view once message last if it isn’t opened?

WhatsApp keeps an unopened view once message for 14 days. A NO SUS Burn File link expires after 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days, whichever you pick.
