DocSend alternative for individuals

DocSend (by Dropbox) is built for business deals and data rooms: paid plans start at $30 per user a month billed yearly, and watermarks need the Advanced plan or above. NO SUS (nosus.foo) watermarked links cover the basics for one person: viewer-email watermark, view log, 30-day links and revoke, free to use today. It isn't end-to-end encrypted.

Android app

How it works in four steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Pick the document

    In the NO SUS app (Android, or app.nosus.foo in any browser), open Send and pick the PDF.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Turn on Watermarked

    Choose Anyone with a link, turn on Watermarked and create the link.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Send it

    The viewer types their email and reads it in a watermarked viewer, with no account and no download button.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Check the log, revoke

    See each view's email, device, time and viewing time, and revoke the link when you're done.

Honest limits

  • Watermarked links are not end-to-end encrypted, and the viewer's email is typed, not verified.
  • DocSend goes much further for teams: detailed analytics, data rooms, eSignatures, NDAs and branding; NO SUS has none of these.
  • A browser can't block screenshots on either service; DocSend says its own screenshot protection can be bypassed.
  • Revoking stops new access on both; copies already captured can't be recalled.

Questions

What is a free DocSend alternative?

NO SUS watermarked links are free to use today and covers a viewer-email watermark, a view log and revoke. DocSend's free Limited Trial allows 10 links and 5 documents, and links that use watermarks or email authentication stop working on it.

Is there an open source DocSend alternative?

Yes. Papermark is open source under the AGPLv3 licence and describes itself as a DocSend alternative with data rooms and analytics. NO SUS is not open source, and it is built for one person, not for teams or data rooms.

How much does DocSend cost?

As of October 2026, DocSend lists Standard at $30 per user a month billed yearly ($43 monthly), Advanced at $150 a month ($250) and Advanced Data Rooms at $180 ($300). Its pricing page offers a free 14-day trial of Advanced Data Rooms.

Does DocSend watermark documents on every plan?

No. DocSend's help center says watermarks and verified-email viewing are only on the Advanced and Advanced Data Rooms plans.

Do viewers need an account on DocSend or NO SUS?

No. DocSend visitors don't need a license to open a link, and NO SUS viewers just type their email in a browser.

Can DocSend or NO SUS stop screenshots?

Neither can guarantee it. DocSend's screenshot protection on Advanced plans blacks out the document when the cursor leaves it, and DocSend says users may find ways around it. NO SUS shows the viewer's email as a watermark and the sender can require a touch-and-hold reveal, which deter but don't block.
