Open your Drive files on a borrowed PC without typing your password

Open nosus.foo/go on the borrowed computer, scan its QR with your phone, match the code and approve with your screen lock. The computer shows only the Saved files you pick, for at most 60 minutes, and never receives your Google password or token. Your Google account stays signed in on your phone only.

How it works in five steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Put the file in Saved

    On your phone, add the file to Saved in the NO SUS app. Saved is a chat with yourself, stored in your own Google Drive.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Open nosus.foo/go

    On the borrowed computer, open nosus.foo/go in any browser. A QR code appears.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Scan and match

    Scan the QR with your phone’s camera and tap the number that matches the one on the computer.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Pick what it can open

    Tick the items and approve with your phone’s screen lock. Nothing else in your Drive is shown.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Close when you’re done

    Close the tab when you finish. Access ends after 60 minutes at most.

Honest limits

  • Go opens items in your NO SUS Saved, not your whole Drive, so add the file to Saved first.
  • Downloads and prints may stay on that computer after the session ends.
  • The computer sees whatever you open on it, so only approve a code shown on the screen in front of you.

Questions

Is incognito mode safe on a public computer?

It helps: Chrome deletes incognito history and cookies when you close all incognito windows. But you still type your password on that machine, and downloaded files stay on it.

Does the borrowed computer get my Google password or token?

No. Your phone stays the only thing signed in to Google. The computer receives only the items you approve, for at most 60 minutes.

Can I open any file in my Drive?

Only items in your NO SUS Saved, which is a chat with yourself stored in your own Google Drive. Add the file to Saved on your phone first.

What happens after 60 minutes?

The session ends and the computer can no longer show your items. Anything already downloaded or printed stays on that computer.

Do I need to install anything on the borrowed PC?

No. You only open nosus.foo/go in its browser.
