40:T2a66,<h2 id="what-did-trai-change-on-18-september-2026">What did TRAI change on 18 September 2026?</h2>
<p>On 18 September 2026 the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India notified the <a href="https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-09/Regulation_18092026.pdf" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2026</a> (05 of 2026) and issued a <a href="https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2311989&lang=1&reg=3" rel="noopener" target="_blank">press release</a> the same day. It amends the 2018 rules behind the DND registry, and press coverage followed around 29 September (<a href="https://www.moneylife.in/article/trai-tightens-spam-rules-aiflagged-callers-face-15day-outgoing-ban-without-consumer-complaint/81794.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Moneylife</a>, <a href="https://www.onmanorama.com/news/business/2026/09/29/trai-directs-telcos-to-act-against-spammers-without-complaints.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Onmanorama</a>, <a href="https://upstox.com/news/business-news/latest-updates/trai-tightens-spam-rules-when-can-telcos-block-callers-and-disconnect-numbers/article-201041/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Upstox</a>).</p>
<p><a href="https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-09/Regulation_18092026.pdf" rel="noopener" target="_blank">TRAI&#39;s explanatory memo</a> explains why complaints alone weren&#39;t enough. In April 2026 operators&#39; AI systems flagged about 771 crore calls and 48 crore SMS as suspected spam, yet customers filed only 2,66,105 complaints, and just 17,401 met the threshold for action. About 89% of complaints from January to April 2026 were against unregistered telemarketers, whose calls typically bypass the DND registry.</p>
<h2 id="how-will-telcos-act-on-spam-they-detect-themselves">How will telcos act on spam they detect themselves?</h2>
<p>A new regulation 21A lets action start from the operators&#39; own AI flags, not only from complaints:</p>
<ol>
<li>The operator receiving a suspicious call flags the number and passes it to the caller&#39;s operator within two hours.</li>
<li>Within one business day, the caller&#39;s operator finds the sender&#39;s KYC details and shares them with all operators, who identify all numbers issued to that sender.</li>
<li>Operators check whether the sender&#39;s other numbers were flagged in the previous ten days. Five or more triggers action.</li>
</ol>
<p>Several headlines called this a 15-day ban for a first offence. <a href="https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-09/Regulation_18092026.pdf" rel="noopener" target="_blank">The regulation text</a> is more graded. A first case means a KYC re-check, with action under the KYC rules if it fails. A second means physical verification, and if the KYC doesn&#39;t match or misuse is confirmed, all outgoing services on all the sender&#39;s SIMs and trunks are barred for 15 days across operators. Later cases can mean one-year disconnection, blacklisting and blocked devices. TRAI&#39;s memo says no sender is penalised on AI flags alone.</p>
<p>Complaints will count for more too. From the 90-day stage, three complaints from different people within ten days, plus an AI flag on any of the sender&#39;s numbers, trigger suspension and an investigation of an unregistered telemarketer. A confirmed first violation brings a 15-day outgoing bar, and a repeat brings one-year disconnection.</p>
<h2 id="do-the-rules-cover-robocalls-and-ai-voice-calls">Do the rules cover robocalls and AI-voice calls?</h2>
<p>The rules now cover application-to-person (A2P) calls: autodialled, robo, pre-recorded and AI-voice calls placed by software. Businesses must declare these calls and their calling numbers to their operator in advance, or the calls count as spam. The receiving operator may charge the caller&#39;s operator up to 5 paise a minute for them, except for calls from the designated 140xx, 1600xx and 1601xx series.</p>
<h2 id="what-happened-to-your-dnd-fully-block-setting">What happened to your DND &quot;FULLY BLOCK&quot; setting?</h2>
<p>FULLY BLOCK is being retired. Every FULLY BLOCK preference moves to BLOCK PROMO within 15 days of the rules coming into force. BLOCK PROMO blocks all promotional categories at all times, but not transactional messages, service messages (OTPs, balance alerts, delivery updates) or government messages. If FULLY BLOCK kept some of those away, expect them back.</p>
<p>The <a href="https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-09/Regulation_18092026.pdf" rel="noopener" target="_blank">amended schedule</a> lists these options:</p>

<p>You can also call 1909 and pick the option at the prompt. Your operator must confirm what it recorded.</p>
<h2 id="when-do-the-new-spam-rules-start">When do the new spam rules start?</h2>
<p>Most provisions apply 30 days after the regulation appears in the Official Gazette. A2P declarations, the 5 paise charge and tighter rules on misused SMS headers follow at 60 days. The consumer appeal route and the matching of complaints with AI data come at 90 days. Neither TRAI&#39;s notification nor the press coverage gives the Gazette publication date. If it was 18 September, the main rules start around 18 October and the last parts around mid-December 2026.</p>
<h2 id="how-do-you-tell-a-real-bank-call-from-a-fake-one">How do you tell a real bank call from a fake one?</h2>
<p>TRAI has reserved number series for regulated callers:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>140xx</strong>: registered promotional telemarketing.</li>
<li><strong>1600xx</strong>: service and transaction calls from banks, insurers and other financial regulated entities, plus government-to-citizen calls. TRAI says only regulated entities get these numbers.</li>
<li><strong>1601xx</strong>: service calls from other sectors, starting with utilities, logistics and courier companies under an August 2026 direction.</li>
</ul>
<p>Once the rules apply, caller-ID apps such as Truecaller may not tag, block or filter these series wholesale. You can still block any number on your own phone.</p>
<p>A &quot;bank&quot; or &quot;courier&quot; call from an ordinary 10-digit number deserves suspicion. It isn&#39;t automatically fake, since TRAI&#39;s memo leaves room to exempt small financial firms from the 1600 mandate and some service calls still use normal numbers. So hang up and call back on the number on your card or in the official app. Whatever number shows, the <a href="https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=53185" rel="noopener" target="_blank">RBI says</a> never to share an OTP, PIN or UPI PIN, even with bank staff, and to ignore calls offering to update your KYC.</p>
<h2 id="what-should-you-do-now">What should you do now?</h2>
<ol data-checklist>
<li><strong>Set your preference.</strong> Text BLOCK PROMO to 1909 or dial <code>*1909*50#</code> and keep the confirmation SMS. If it isn&#39;t accepted yet, retry after the rules take effect. FULLY BLOCK users should check their setting after the migration.</li>
<li><strong>Check the number.</strong> A &quot;bank&quot; on an ordinary mobile number gets a call back on the official number, never your OTP.</li>
<li><strong>Complain within seven days, in the right place.</strong> Use the DND app, 1909 or your operator&#39;s app, with the full number or SMS header. After seven days it is logged only as a report. A &quot;report spam&quot; tap in a caller-ID app reaches no operator unless the app forwards it to the operators&#39; DLT platform, which the new rules require. TRAI&#39;s memo, citing a Truecaller report, notes one spammer was reported over 3.25 lakh times in the app during 2025 and still made about 1.25 crore calls.</li>
<li><strong>Report fraud on Chakshu.</strong> Fake KYC, bank or government calls, SMS and WhatsApp messages go to <a href="https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/sfc/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Chakshu on Sanchar Saathi</a> within 30 days. If you&#39;ve lost money, call 1930 or file at cybercrime.gov.in immediately.</li>
<li><strong>Appeal a wrongly closed complaint.</strong> From the 90-day stage, you can appeal within 15 days of the operator&#39;s reply, or, if it doesn&#39;t reply within 15 days, during the following 15 days. <a href="https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2311989&lang=1&reg=3" rel="noopener" target="_blank">TRAI&#39;s release</a> says appeals use the same channels as complaints: the DND app, your operator&#39;s app or portal, or 1909.</li>
<li><strong>Hand out your number less.</strong> Under the amended rules, a written or online enquiry lets a business contact you for seven days, and an application for three months. Every form you fill and every &quot;send it on WhatsApp&quot; request gives your number to one more business. See <a href="/blog/print-shop-document-privacy/">print shop document privacy</a> and <a href="/blog/receive-files-without-sharing-your-phone-number/">receiving files without sharing your number</a>.</li>
</ol>
