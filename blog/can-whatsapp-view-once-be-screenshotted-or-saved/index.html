They can still be photographed with another phone. So view once stops casual saving, not a determined recipient. What WhatsApp says view once blocks

WhatsApp's About view once page and its how-to page describe these limits for photos, videos and voice messages:

  • The recipient cannot forward, copy, save, star or share view once media.
  • The recipient cannot take a screenshot of a view once photo or video. The how-to page also says screen recording is blocked, in the same words for Android and iPhone.
  • After the recipient opens it and leaves the viewer, it cannot be opened again.
  • It is not saved to the camera roll or gallery.
  • If nobody opens it within 14 days of sending, it expires.
  • Opened view once media is left out of chat backups, so it cannot be restored. An unopened one can still be in a backup.
  • Encrypted media may stay on WhatsApp's servers for a few weeks after sending.

WhatsApp announced view once in a 2021 blog post. It added view once for voice messages in December 2023.

What it does not block

WhatsApp is open about two gaps.

  1. Another camera. A recipient can take a photo or video of the screen with a second phone or camera before the media disappears. This is the real limit, and it applies to every app that shows content on a screen.
  2. Audio via the screen recorder. WhatsApp says iOS and Android users may be able to record the audio of a view once voice message with the phone's own screen recorder.

Nothing in WhatsApp's pages mentions an alert to the sender when someone tries to capture the screen. The sender only sees an Opened receipt, and only if the recipient has read receipts on.

Was screenshot blocking ever "changed"? What we can and cannot confirm

The screenshot block did not arrive everywhere at once, so older articles can contradict newer ones.

  • August 2022: WhatsApp's blog said it was testing a way to stop screenshots of view once content.
  • 4 October 2022: Android Police reported an Android beta where the attempt showed a "security policy" message, with the sender not notified.
  • 15 December 2022: Lowyat.NET reported blocking on Android and iPhone. On iPhone, the screenshot showed a "Screenshot Blocked" page instead of the photo. People on older app versions could still screenshot.

What is documented: WhatsApp's current help pages describe the block. What is not documented: WhatsApp does not publish which app versions, phones or operating system releases enforce it, and its two help pages differ on whether they mention screen recording. We found no credible report from 2025 or 2026 saying the block was removed. Some SEO guides sell "workarounds", but we treat those as unverified.

If it matters, update WhatsApp on both phones and assume the block can fail.

Can view once be recovered?

WhatsApp documents no way to bring back opened view once media. It is excluded from backups, and WhatsApp says it cannot be viewed again. If you see an app or website promising to "recover" view once photos, be careful. Handing a third party access to your account is a bigger risk than the photo you lost.

When is view once enough?

View once is good for low-stakes things: a photo of a menu, a parking spot, a quick shot of a bill you do not want in someone's gallery. It stops the photo piling up in a chat, in a backup or in a cousin's phone gallery.

It is not enough when:

  • The recipient might copy it. Another phone defeats it.
  • The content is an ID or financial document. Do not send an Aadhaar or PAN scan this way. Read how to share an Aadhaar copy safely first.
  • It is a PDF or a bundle of files. View once does not support them. A normal WhatsApp document stays in the chat. See how to send a PDF that opens once.

The short version

WhatsApp view once is built to stop saving from inside the app, and it largely does for photos and videos. It was never built to stop a second phone. Use it for photos you would be fine with someone briefly seeing, and send anything more serious in a way that does not depend on the recipient behaving.

Questions people ask

Can you screenshot WhatsApp view once?

No. WhatsApp's help pages say you cannot take a screenshot of a view once photo or video you receive. Reports from 2022 show the block was rolled out in stages and older app versions could still screenshot, and WhatsApp does not publish a version list, so a block is likely but not guaranteed.

Can WhatsApp view once be screen recorded?

Not for photos and videos. WhatsApp's how-to page says recipients cannot take a screen recording of view once media. The exception it documents is audio: iOS and Android users may be able to record a view once voice message with the phone's native screen recorder.

Can you screen record a view once video on WhatsApp?

No. View once videos follow the same rules as view once photos: no screenshot, no screen recording, no forwarding or saving, and no second viewing once the recipient leaves the viewer. Only voice messages are different, because the phone's own screen recorder may capture their audio.

Is it different on Android and iPhone?

No. WhatsApp's how-to page gives the same rule in its Android and iOS versions: recipients cannot screenshot or screen record view once photos and videos. On both, WhatsApp says the native screen recorder may capture the audio of a view once voice message.

What happens if you try to screenshot view once?

WhatsApp's help pages do not describe what the recipient sees. Reports from 2022 did: an Android beta showed a security policy message, and on iPhone the screenshot showed a Screenshot Blocked page instead of the photo.

Does WhatsApp tell the sender if someone tries to screenshot view once?

WhatsApp's help pages describe no alert to the sender, and tech reports from 2022 said the sender is not notified when a screenshot attempt is blocked. What the sender can see is an Opened receipt, and only if the recipient has read receipts turned on.

Can someone photograph a view once photo with another phone?

Yes. WhatsApp itself warns that a recipient can take a photo or video of view once media with a camera or another device before it disappears. This is the one limit no app setting fixes.

Can you save or recover a WhatsApp view once photo after opening it?

No, according to WhatsApp. Opened view once media cannot be viewed again, is not saved to the gallery, and is left out of backups, so it cannot be restored. WhatsApp documents no recovery method, and tools that promise one deserve suspicion.

Do view once recorder apps work?

We have not verified any. WhatsApp documents no way to record, save or recover view once photos and videos, and says screen recording them is blocked. Handing a third-party app access to your WhatsApp is a bigger risk than the photo.

Can you send a PDF as view once on WhatsApp?

No. View once covers photos, videos and voice messages only. For a document that opens once, you can paste a one-time link into the chat instead.
