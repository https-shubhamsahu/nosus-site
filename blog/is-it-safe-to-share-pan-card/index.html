The short answer

Usually yes, when a real transaction needs it. PAN is meant to be quoted to banks, employers, brokers and sellers of big-ticket items, so the number itself isn't a secret. The risk is a full copy, with your photo, date of birth and signature, in the hands of someone who applies for credit in your name. Share it only when a deal needs it, write the purpose on the copy, and check your Annual Information Statement and credit report for anything you don't recognise.

PAN is meant to be quoted, not posted. Your Permanent Account Number is how the Income Tax Department ties big transactions to you, so banks, employers and sellers are supposed to ask for it. Whether sharing it is safe depends on who is asking and what you send.

Is it safe to share your PAN card number?

The number on its own, yes, with anyone who needs it for a real transaction. The tax rules have long made PAN compulsory for a list of transactions so that they can be reported against your name (PIB). A PAN can't move money by itself: there is no PIN or OTP attached to it.

The card is a different matter. A PAN card copy also shows your photo, your date of birth, your parent's name and your signature. Sent together with an Aadhaar copy, a selfie and a cancelled cheque, it's most of what a fraudster needs to try a loan in your name with a lender that doesn't verify properly. That combination, not the number, is what to guard.

Who can genuinely ask for your PAN?

Under the old Rule 114B, the list of transactions that need PAN included (PIB 2016, PIB 2015):

  • opening a bank account (other than a basic or Jan Dhan account), or a time deposit over ₹50,000 (or more than ₹5 lakh in a year)
  • depositing more than ₹50,000 in cash at a bank in one day
  • paying more than ₹50,000 in cash for a hotel or restaurant bill at one time
  • buying or selling property worth more than ₹10 lakh
  • buying or selling anything worth more than ₹2 lakh, however you pay

Employers, mutual funds and stockbrokers usually ask for it too. A new Income-tax Act took over from the 1961 Act on 1 April 2026, with new rules, so some limits may have changed. Check the current list on the e-filing portal before you argue at a counter.

Who has no need for your PAN: a recruiter before an interview, a delivery agent, a "customer care" caller, a lottery or prize message, or a loan agent who isn't working from a lender's own app.

How to share your PAN card safely online

  1. Send the e-PAN PDF, not a phone photo. The e-PAN is the official digital card. If you applied through Protean, it can be emailed to you; it is free up to three times within 30 days of allotment or an update, and ₹8.26 after that (Protean). If you don't have a PAN yet, the e-filing portal's Instant e-PAN is free if your Aadhaar has a linked mobile and you can use DigiLocker.
  2. Write the purpose and date on it. "For KYC with [bank], [date] only", running across the photo edge. Our PDF watermark guide shows how on a phone.
  3. Use the requester's channel. Their portal or an email address on their official domain, not a personal WhatsApp number.
  4. Send only what's asked. Don't attach PAN, Aadhaar and a cheque together unless the form asks for all three.
  5. Never post it. Not in a group, not in a public comment asking for help with a refund.

There is no official masked PAN that we could find, unlike masked Aadhaar, so the purpose note is your main protection. PAN 2.0, approved in 2024, adds a dynamic QR code; its FAQ mentions no masked PAN, and existing holders don't need a new PAN (PIB).

How to check if your PAN has been misused

Your Annual Information Statement. Log in to the income tax e-filing portal and open AIS. It is the department's "comprehensive view of information" reported against your PAN: interest, dividends, large purchases and more. Form 26AS on TRACES now shows only tax deducted or collected at source. If AIS shows something that isn't yours, you can give feedback on that entry.

Your credit report. Every credit bureau must give you one free full credit report, with your score, each calendar year. Each bureau gives its own, so you can spread them across the year. Look for loans, cards or enquiries you don't recognise. Since 2024, RBI also requires credit bureaus to send you an SMS or email when a lender pulls your report, so an unexpected "your credit report was accessed" alert is worth acting on.

Banks themselves must verify identity documents against the original or through digital checks, not just file a copy (RBI KYC directions). The alerts and the free reports are there because not every lender is that careful.

What to do if your PAN is misused

  1. Report it to the police or cyber cell first: file at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 (PIB). If your income tax e-filing account was misused, the department's misuse page says to report to the police first and attach that complaint when you write to it.
  2. Contact the lender named on your credit report, in writing, and say the loan isn't yours.
  3. Raise a dispute with the credit bureau that shows it.
  4. Change your e-filing portal password and check its registered mobile and email.
  5. Keep copies of every complaint and reply.

What these steps don't do

  • A purpose note on a copy discourages reuse; it can't stop someone determined to forge.
  • AIS and credit reports show misuse after it happens. They don't block it.
  • Nothing recalls a PAN copy you have already sent.

Questions people ask

Is PAN card sharing safe?

Yes, with a bank, employer, broker or seller who needs it for a transaction the tax rules cover. It's riskier with strangers, loan agents on WhatsApp or public groups, because a copy also shows your photo, date of birth and signature.

Is sharing your PAN card number safe?

The number is designed to be quoted on forms and invoices, so sharing it with a business that needs it is normal. Avoid sending a PAN copy together with Aadhaar, a selfie and a cheque unless a regulated lender's own app asks for them.

How do I share my PAN card safely online?

Send the e-PAN PDF rather than a phone photo, write who it is for and the date on it, and upload it only through the requester's official portal or email. Never post it in a group or a public comment.

Is there a masked PAN like masked Aadhaar?

We couldn't find one. The Income Tax Department and its PAN agencies offer e-PAN, but no official version that hides digits the way masked Aadhaar does, so marking each copy with its purpose matters more.

How can I check if my PAN card is misused?

Log in to the income tax e-filing portal and read your Annual Information Statement for transactions you didn't make. Also get your free annual credit report from each credit bureau and look for loans or enquiries you don't recognise.

What should I do if someone misused my PAN?

Report it at cybercrime.gov.in or on 1930, then dispute any unknown loan with the lender and the credit bureau. If your income tax e-filing account was misused, the department asks you to report to the police first and attach that complaint when you write to it.
