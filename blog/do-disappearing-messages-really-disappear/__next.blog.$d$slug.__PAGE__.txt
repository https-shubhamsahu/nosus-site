39:T3894,<p>A disappearing message does one job well: it removes the message from the chat on both phones after a timer. That helps when a phone is lost, borrowed by a sibling, checked by a parent, or handed to a repair shop. What it cannot do is control the other person&#39;s side while the message is still on their screen. Every major app admits this in its own help pages.</p>
<p>Below is what WhatsApp, Instagram, Snapchat, Telegram and Signal actually do as of October 2026, based on their official help centres. If you only want to know which app to pick, see <a href="/blog/best-self-destructing-message-apps/">the best disappearing message apps</a>.</p>
<h2 id="where-does-a-disappearing-message-survive">Where does a disappearing message survive?</h2>
<p>The same leaks show up in all five apps:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Another phone&#39;s camera.</strong> WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram and Signal all warn that the recipient can photograph the screen with a second device. No app setting can stop this.</li>
<li><strong>Screenshots and screen recordings.</strong> Some apps block them for certain message types, some send you an alert, and some do neither. The table below shows which.</li>
<li><strong>Notification previews.</strong> The text can appear in a notification before anyone opens the app. <a href="https://faq.whatsapp.com/673193694148537/?locale=en_US" rel="noopener" target="_blank">WhatsApp says</a> a preview might stay visible after the message is gone, until the person opens WhatsApp. Many Android phones also have <a href="https://support.google.com/android/answer/9079661?hl=en" rel="noopener" target="_blank">notification history</a>, which lists recently dismissed notifications and the last 24 hours of them.</li>
<li><strong>Backups.</strong> A backup taken before the timer ran out can hold the message.</li>
<li><strong>Quotes, forwards and &quot;keep&quot; buttons.</strong> A reply that quotes the message, a forward into a normal chat, or a saved message can outlast the original.</li>
<li><strong>Other logged-in screens.</strong> A WhatsApp Web session left open on a college lab PC is one more place the chat is visible while the message exists. If that worries you, see <a href="/blog/logout-whatsapp-web-from-another-computer/">how to log out of WhatsApp Web from another computer</a>.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="whatsapp-disappearing-messages-and-view-once">WhatsApp: disappearing messages and view once</h2>
<p><a href="https://faq.whatsapp.com/673193694148537/?locale=en_US" rel="noopener" target="_blank">WhatsApp&#39;s disappearing messages</a> can be set to 24 hours, 7 days or 90 days. Either person in a one-to-one chat can switch it on, and you can make it the default for new chats under Settings &gt; Privacy &gt; Default message timer. It only affects messages sent after you change the setting.</p>
<p>The gaps WhatsApp lists itself:</p>
<ul>
<li>Anyone in the chat can <strong>keep</strong> a message before it expires. The sender gets a notification and can unkeep it.</li>
<li>A message <strong>forwarded</strong> to a chat without disappearing messages stays there.</li>
<li>A <strong>quoted reply</strong> can stay visible after the original has gone.</li>
<li>A <strong>backup</strong> made before the message disappears includes it, though disappearing messages are deleted when someone restores from that backup.</li>
</ul>
<p>Media in a disappearing chat is not saved to the gallery.</p>
<p><a href="https://faq.whatsapp.com/1077018839582332/?locale=en_US" rel="noopener" target="_blank">View once</a> is stricter. The recipient cannot forward, copy, save or star the photo, video or voice note, and <a href="https://faq.whatsapp.com/578442220724722/?locale=en_US" rel="noopener" target="_blank">cannot screenshot or screen-record</a> view once photos or videos. WhatsApp still warns that someone can film the screen with another device, that phone screen recorders can capture the audio of view once voice messages, and that encrypted media may stay on its servers for a few weeks. More on this in <a href="/blog/can-whatsapp-view-once-be-screenshotted-or-saved/">can WhatsApp view once be screenshotted or screen recorded</a>.</p>
<p>For sensitive chats, WhatsApp also has <a href="https://blog.whatsapp.com/introducing-advanced-chat-privacy" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Advanced Chat Privacy</a> (launched April 2025). Tap the chat name, then Advanced Chat Privacy. It stops others from exporting the chat, auto-downloading its media to their phone, or using its messages for AI features. WhatsApp&#39;s announcement does not say it blocks screenshots.</p>
<h2 id="instagram-vanish-mode-and-disappearing-messages">Instagram: vanish mode and disappearing messages</h2>
<p>In <a href="https://help.instagram.com/android-app/888592124998543" rel="noopener" target="_blank">vanish mode</a>, you swipe up in a chat, and messages vanish when someone leaves the chat or turns the mode off. Nobody can copy, save or forward them. Screenshots and screen recordings are still possible: Instagram sends you a notification if it detects a screenshot, and it warns that someone can also photograph the screen with another device. Vanish mode may not be available in Europe.</p>
<p>Instagram also documents a separate, timer-based <a href="https://help.instagram.com/android-app/708011878194232/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">disappearing messages</a> option in some locations. Under it, you can only forward or save messages you sent yourself. Some message types aren&#39;t supported and won&#39;t disappear (they show an info icon). A reaction can change when a message disappears, and the option doesn&#39;t work in group chats.</p>
<h2 id="snapchat-deletion-is-the-default-saving-takes-one-tap">Snapchat: deletion is the default, saving takes one tap</h2>
<p>According to <a href="https://help.snapchat.com/hc/en-us/articles/7012334940948-When-does-Snapchat-delete-Snaps-and-Chats" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Snapchat Support</a>, Chats are deleted 24 hours after everyone has viewed them, or 31 days after sending, whichever comes first. Anyone in the conversation can change this to After Viewing, 7 Days After Viewing or Never, and the new setting applies only to messages sent after the change. Everyone sees a notice when someone changes it.</p>
<p>Saving, replying to or reacting to a message keeps it longer. Snaps can be saved into the chat while you watch them or straight after. If you delete a Snap from the chat, Snapchat tries to remove it from your friends&#39; devices too, but says this may fail if they have a bad connection or an old app version.</p>
<p>The app shows <a href="https://help.snapchat.com/hc/en-us/articles/7012315702548-What-do-the-icons-on-the-Chat-Screen-mean" rel="noopener" target="_blank">a screenshot icon</a> when someone screenshots your Snap or Chat. An icon is a notice, not a block. Snap&#39;s <a href="https://help.snapchat.com/hc/en-us/articles/7121497973140-How-do-screenshots-work-on-Snapchat-for-Web" rel="noopener" target="_blank">own guidance</a> is blunt: whether someone uses the app or Snapchat on a computer, the people who view your Snaps and Chats can always capture and copy them.</p>
<h2 id="telegram-secret-chats-vs-normal-chats">Telegram: secret chats vs normal chats</h2>
<p><a href="https://telegram.org/faq" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Secret chats</a> are end-to-end encrypted, can&#39;t be forwarded, and live only on the device where they were started, not in Telegram&#39;s cloud. The self-destruct timer starts when the message appears on the recipient&#39;s screen. Telegram says it will try to send a screenshot alert, but admits there is no bulletproof way to detect screenshots on some systems, naming some Android devices, and that alerts can be bypassed.</p>
<p>Normal Telegram chats are cloud chats, stored encrypted on Telegram&#39;s servers. Telegram added an <a href="https://telegram.org/blog/autodelete-inv2" rel="noopener" target="_blank">auto-delete timer</a> for them in 2021, and it counts from when a message is sent, not when it is read.</p>
<p>The newer option is <a href="https://telegram.org/blog/member-tags-disable-sharing-and-more" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Disable Sharing</a>, added in March 2026 and limited to Telegram Premium users at launch. Turn it on from a contact&#39;s profile (⋯ or ⋮ &gt; Disable Sharing). In that 1-on-1 chat it blocks forwarding, screenshots and saving media. A camera still gets around it.</p>
<h2 id="signal-designed-for-tidiness-not-for-enemies">Signal: designed for tidiness, not for enemies</h2>
<p>Signal is the most honest about the limits. Its <a href="https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/360007320771-Set-and-manage-disappearing-messages" rel="noopener" target="_blank">help page</a> says the feature is &quot;not for situations where your contact is your adversary.&quot; Anyone in the chat can set a timer of up to 4 weeks, it syncs to your linked devices, and the message is deleted from disk when the timer ends.</p>
<p>Signal&#39;s help pages describe no screenshot alert. Its <a href="https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/360043469312-Screen-Security" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Screen Security</a> setting blocks screenshots on your own phone only, not on the other person&#39;s, and Signal Desktop blocks them on Windows 11 by default so Microsoft Recall can&#39;t capture your messages. <a href="https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/9708267671322-Signal-Secure-Backups" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Secure Backups</a> leave out view-once messages and anything due to disappear in the next 24 hours, so a message on a longer timer can still land in a backup.</p>
<h2 id="how-to-make-disappearing-messages-leak-less">How to make disappearing messages leak less</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Set the timer before you send.</strong> In WhatsApp, Signal and Telegram, a new timer only applies to new messages.</li>
<li><strong>Hide message text in notifications.</strong> In Signal on Android, tap your profile &gt; Notifications &gt; Show and choose <a href="https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/360043273491-In-App-Notification-Options" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Name only or No name or message</a>. On Android, turn off <a href="https://support.google.com/android/answer/9079661?hl=en" rel="noopener" target="_blank">sensitive notifications on the lock screen</a> in your notification settings.</li>
<li><strong>Use view once for photos on WhatsApp</strong>, and turn on Advanced Chat Privacy for chats you care about.</li>
<li><strong>Don&#39;t quote-reply to sensitive messages.</strong> The quote can outlive the original.</li>
<li><strong>Check linked devices</strong> and log out of WhatsApp Web or Telegram Desktop on shared computers.</li>
<li><strong>Keep secrets out of chat entirely.</strong> Passwords and OTPs belong elsewhere; see <a href="/blog/how-to-send-a-password-securely/">how to send a password securely</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Judge by the person, not the timer.</strong> If you wouldn&#39;t trust them with a permanent copy, don&#39;t send it.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="where-no-sus-fits">Where NO SUS fits</h2>
<p>When you don&#39;t want the content sitting in a chat at all, a one-time link can help. A NO SUS <a href="/guides/#burn">Burn Note or Burn File</a> is encrypted in your browser and opens once, then it&#39;s gone; you pick the expiry. After it&#39;s opened, the chat and any backup hold only a dead link. The limits: until it&#39;s opened, the link itself carries the key, so whoever opens it first gets the content. Whoever opens it can also screenshot or photograph it, and a browser cannot block screenshots (the site only uses blur and watermarks as a deterrent). You can <a href="/#try">try it without an account</a>, or compare options in our <a href="/blog/self-destructing-note-tools-compared/">self-destructing note tools guide</a>.</p>
<h2 id="so-do-disappearing-messages-work">So, do disappearing messages work?</h2>
<p>They work for what they were built for: fewer old messages on your phone and theirs, so a lost or snooped phone reveals less. They are not a lock against the person you&#39;re talking to. Screenshot alerts and blocks are uneven, backups and previews can lag behind, and every app concedes that the person on the other end can keep a copy. Use the timer to keep things tidy, and choose recipients as if every message were permanent.</p>
