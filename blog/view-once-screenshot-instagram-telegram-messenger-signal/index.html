Can someone screenshot view once on Instagram?

Instagram's help centre gives two answers. Its page on the status of a photo you sent says "taking a screenshot is now disabled" for a disappearing photo or video sent in a chat. Its pages on sending and replaying still say the sender can see if a screenshot was detected. The newer line reads like the current rule, but Instagram hasn't cleaned up the old one, so treat the block as likely, not certain.

How it works: before sending, pick View once, View twice (one replay, right after it arrives) or Unlimited views. You can only send one to people who follow you or have approved your messages, and it works in the mobile app only, not on computers.

Vanish mode is different. There, Instagram says people can screenshot or screen-record, and you get a notification if a screenshot is detected.

Can someone screenshot view once on Telegram?

Telegram's self-destruct timer works on photos and videos in one-to-one chats, and on everything in secret chats. In the media editor you choose how long the photo stays visible after opening, "from View Once to 30 seconds". Voice and video messages can be view-once too.

On screenshots, Telegram's 2023 announcement says saving and taking screenshots "will not work" for self-destructing media. Its FAQ is more modest: it "will try" to send a notification if a screenshot is taken. In secret chats Telegram says it tries to alert you, but admits the alerts can be bypassed.

Telegram Premium users also have Disable Sharing (March 2026), which blocks screenshots and saving media in a one-to-one chat.

Can someone screenshot view once on Messenger?

No, according to Meta. On media sent as view once, "others won't be able to save, forward, screenshot or screen record" it. To send one, tap Keep in chat under your photo until it says View once. The feature is "being gradually introduced", so you may not have it yet, and it isn't available on computers.

In end-to-end encrypted chats, Messenger's disappearing messages page says that if it detects a screenshot or screen recording of an end-to-end encrypted chat, everyone in it is notified, and warns that it may not catch them all. Meta announced in 2022 that it was removing vanish mode from Messenger.

Can someone screenshot view-once photos on Signal?

Signal doesn't say. Its View Once Media page describes photos and videos that are deleted from the chat after they're viewed, in one-to-one and group chats, on phones and Signal Desktop. It mentions no screenshot block and no alert.

The setting people mix up with this is Screen Security. It stops screenshots of Signal on your own device only, and Signal says plainly that it does not stop someone else taking one on theirs.

One number worth knowing: an unopened view-once photo is deleted 45 days after it was sent, or sooner if a disappearing-message timer runs out first.

Is there a view once photo screenshot app?

Apps and videos that promise to save view once photos are common. We have not verified any, and none of these companies documents a way around its own block. An app that wants access to your WhatsApp, Instagram or Telegram account is a bigger risk than the photo you'd save. The one method every app admits works is the plainest: another phone's camera.

What view once can't stop

  • A photo or video of the screen, taken with another device. WhatsApp's view once page and Signal's disappearing-messages page both say so.
  • Someone who reads it aloud, retypes it or remembers it.
  • Screenshots where the app only notifies: a notice tells you after the fact, it doesn't stop the copy.

Questions people ask

Can someone screenshot view once on Instagram?

Instagram's help pages disagree. One says taking a screenshot is now disabled for a disappearing photo or video sent in a chat; two others still say the sender can see if a screenshot was detected. Either way, a photo taken with another phone is not blocked or reported.

Can you screenshot view once on Telegram?

Telegram's 2023 announcement says saving and taking screenshots will not work for view-once and other self-destructing media. Its FAQ is more careful: it will try to send a notification if a screenshot is taken. A camera on another device still works.

Can someone screenshot view once on Messenger?

No, according to Meta. Messenger's help page says others won't be able to save, forward, screenshot or screen record view once media. The feature is still being introduced gradually, so not everyone has it yet.

Does Signal notify you if someone screenshots a view-once photo?

Signal's help pages describe no screenshot alert and no block for view-once media. Its Screen Security setting blocks screenshots on your own phone only, not on the other person's.

Can someone screen record a view once photo?

WhatsApp and Messenger say screen recording view once photos and videos is blocked. Instagram's and Signal's view-once pages don't mention screen recording. WhatsApp adds that the audio of a view once voice message may be recorded with the phone's own screen recorder.

Is there a view once photo screenshot app?

We have not verified any, and none of these apps documents a way around its own block. An app that asks for access to your messaging account is a bigger risk than the photo. The one method every app admits works is another phone's camera.
