<h2 id="can-someone-screenshot-view-once-on-instagram">Can someone screenshot view once on Instagram?</h2>
<p>Instagram&#39;s help centre gives two answers. Its page on the status of a photo you sent says <a href="https://help.instagram.com/272077316522642/?cms_platform=android-app" rel="noopener" target="_blank">&quot;taking a screenshot is now disabled&quot;</a> for a disappearing photo or video sent in a chat. Its pages on <a href="https://help.instagram.com/1310346208996329/?cms_platform=android-app" rel="noopener" target="_blank">sending</a> and <a href="https://help.instagram.com/640455899468439/?cms_platform=android-app" rel="noopener" target="_blank">replaying</a> still say the sender can see if a screenshot was detected. The newer line reads like the current rule, but Instagram hasn&#39;t cleaned up the old one, so treat the block as likely, not certain.</p>
<p>How it works: before sending, pick <strong>View once</strong>, <strong>View twice</strong> (one replay, right after it arrives) or <strong>Unlimited views</strong>. You can only send one to people who follow you or have approved your messages, and it works in the mobile app only, not on computers.</p>
<p>Vanish mode is different. There, Instagram says people <a href="https://help.instagram.com/888592124998543/?cms_platform=android-app" rel="noopener" target="_blank">can screenshot or screen-record</a>, and you get a notification if a screenshot is detected.</p>
<h2 id="can-someone-screenshot-view-once-on-telegram">Can someone screenshot view once on Telegram?</h2>
<p>Telegram&#39;s <a href="https://telegram.org/faq" rel="noopener" target="_blank">self-destruct timer</a> works on photos and videos in one-to-one chats, and on everything in secret chats. In the media editor you choose how long the photo stays visible after opening, <a href="https://telegram.org/blog/channel-stories" rel="noopener" target="_blank">&quot;from View Once to 30 seconds&quot;</a>. <a href="https://telegram.org/blog/new-saved-messages-and-9-more" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Voice and video messages</a> can be view-once too.</p>
<p>On screenshots, Telegram&#39;s 2023 announcement says saving and taking screenshots &quot;will not work&quot; for self-destructing media. Its FAQ is more modest: it &quot;will try&quot; to send a notification if a screenshot is taken. In secret chats Telegram says it tries to alert you, but admits the alerts can be bypassed.</p>
<p>Telegram Premium users also have <a href="https://telegram.org/blog/member-tags-disable-sharing-and-more" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Disable Sharing</a> (March 2026), which blocks screenshots and saving media in a one-to-one chat.</p>
<h2 id="can-someone-screenshot-view-once-on-messenger">Can someone screenshot view once on Messenger?</h2>
<p>No, according to Meta. On media sent <a href="https://www.facebook.com/help/messenger-app/1692866107948379/?cms_platform=android-app" rel="noopener" target="_blank">as view once</a>, &quot;others won&#39;t be able to save, forward, screenshot or screen record&quot; it. To send one, tap <strong>Keep in chat</strong> under your photo until it says <strong>View once</strong>. The feature is &quot;being gradually introduced&quot;, so you may not have it yet, and it isn&#39;t available on computers.</p>
<p>In end-to-end encrypted chats, Messenger&#39;s <a href="https://www.facebook.com/help/messenger-app/1039542879410863/?cms_platform=android-app" rel="noopener" target="_blank">disappearing messages page</a> says that if it detects a screenshot or screen recording of an end-to-end encrypted chat, everyone in it is notified, and warns that it may not catch them all. Meta <a href="https://about.fb.com/news/2022/08/testing-end-to-end-encrypted-backups-and-more-on-messenger/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">announced in 2022</a> that it was removing vanish mode from Messenger.</p>
<h2 id="can-someone-screenshot-view-once-photos-on-signal">Can someone screenshot view-once photos on Signal?</h2>
<p>Signal doesn&#39;t say. Its <a href="https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/360038443071-View-Once-Media" rel="noopener" target="_blank">View Once Media page</a> describes photos and videos that are deleted from the chat after they&#39;re viewed, in one-to-one and group chats, on phones and Signal Desktop. It mentions no screenshot block and no alert.</p>
<p>The setting people mix up with this is <a href="https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/360043469312-Screen-Security" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Screen Security</a>. It stops screenshots of Signal on your own device only, and Signal says plainly that it does not stop someone else taking one on theirs.</p>
<p>One number worth knowing: an unopened view-once photo is deleted 45 days after it was sent, or sooner if a disappearing-message timer runs out first.</p>
<h2 id="is-there-a-view-once-photo-screenshot-app">Is there a view once photo screenshot app?</h2>
<p>Apps and videos that promise to save view once photos are common. We have not verified any, and none of these companies documents a way around its own block. An app that wants access to your WhatsApp, Instagram or Telegram account is a bigger risk than the photo you&#39;d save. The one method every app admits works is the plainest: another phone&#39;s camera.</p>
