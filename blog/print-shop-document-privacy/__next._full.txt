46:T3182,<p>At a print shop, the bigger risk is usually convenience, not hacking. The file goes to the shopkeeper&#39;s WhatsApp, gets downloaded onto the shop computer so it can be printed, and then sits in four or five places nobody ever cleans. Fixing that is mostly about choosing how the file reaches the printer, plus two minutes of cleanup before you pay.</p>
<h2 id="where-your-documents-leak-at-a-xerox-or-print-shop">Where your documents leak at a Xerox or print shop</h2>
<p><strong>The shopkeeper&#39;s WhatsApp.</strong> It&#39;s the easiest option and the leakiest. Your Aadhaar, PAN card or marksheet now lives in a chat on someone else&#39;s phone, often in its storage too, and on the shop PC if they print through WhatsApp Web. Your phone number goes along with it. <a href="https://www.medianama.com/2025/12/223-explained-uidais-new-rule-aadhaar-based-verification/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">MediaNama&#39;s explainer</a> on UIDAI&#39;s December 2025 plan to curb photocopies names this exact risk: ID copies kept on phones and in unprotected folders that nobody plans to clear.</p>
<p><strong>Accounts left logged in.</strong> If you open WhatsApp Web or Gmail on the shop computer to fetch your file, the session does not end when you walk away. WhatsApp&#39;s own instructions for disconnecting WhatsApp Web are to log out on the computer itself, or from <strong>Linked devices</strong> on your phone (<a href="https://faq.whatsapp.com/834124628020911/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">WhatsApp Help Center</a>). The full walkthrough is in our guide to <a href="/blog/logout-whatsapp-web-from-another-computer/">logging out of WhatsApp Web from another computer</a>.</p>
<p><strong>Downloads and the Recycle Bin.</strong> Anything opened from a chat, email or link usually lands in the Downloads folder. Private windows don&#39;t fix this: <a href="https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/9845881?hl=en" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Chrome&#39;s Incognito help page</a> says downloaded files stay on the device until someone deletes them, even after the window is closed. Deleting is only half the job, because a deleted file can sometimes be recovered until its space is overwritten, which is what Microsoft&#39;s <a href="https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/recover-lost-files-on-windows-10-61f5b28a-f5b8-3cc2-0f8e-a63cb4e1d4c4" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Windows File Recovery</a> tool is built to do. A May 2022 government advisory told people to permanently delete any e-Aadhaar downloaded on a cafe or kiosk computer. The advisory was withdrawn within days over fears it could be misread (<a href="https://businesstoday.in/latest/trends/story/govt-withdraws-its-recent-aadhaar-advisory-says-exercise-normal-prudence-335558-2022-05-29" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Business Today</a>), but the cleanup advice still holds.</p>
<p><strong>e-Aadhaar PDFs.</strong> The e-Aadhaar file is password protected, but <a href="https://uidai.gov.in/en/aadhaar-online-services" rel="noopener" target="_blank">UIDAI sets that password</a> as the first four letters of your name in capitals plus your year of birth. Anyone who saw your printout knows both. An e-Aadhaar left in Downloads is as good as open.</p>
<p><strong>Your pen drive.</strong> USB drives are a classic way for malware to move between computers. <a href="https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/news/using-caution-usb-drives" rel="noopener" target="_blank">CISA explains</a> that an infected computer can copy malicious code onto a drive plugged into it, which then infects the next computer the drive goes into. A shop PC that many strangers plug into is a poor place to test your luck. It works the other way too: the shopkeeper can browse every file on your drive, not only the one you came to print.</p>
<p><strong>The machine and the extra pages.</strong> Many office copiers have a built-in hard drive, and the US Federal Trade Commission warns that it <a href="https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/digital-copier-data-security-guide-businesses" rel="noopener" target="_blank">keeps data on the jobs the machine handles</a>, including copies, prints and scans. You cannot wipe that yourself, which is one more reason to send fewer pages through. Then there are misprints, test pages and the &quot;one extra copy, just in case&quot;, which end up in a dustbin or a drawer.</p>
<h2 id="safer-ways-to-print-a-document-ranked">Safer ways to print a document, ranked</h2>
<p class="table-hint" aria-hidden="true"></p><div class="table-scroll" role="region" aria-label="Table" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Rank</th><th scope="col">Method</th><th scope="col">What can stay behind</th><th scope="col">Catch</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><th scope="row">1</th><td>Photocopy from the paper original</td><td>Nothing on the PC (copier memory aside)</td><td>Only works if you have the paper</td></tr><tr><th scope="row">2</th><td>Print straight from your phone to the printer</td><td>Nothing on the shop PC</td><td>Printer must support it, and the shop must allow it</td></tr><tr><th scope="row">3</th><td>A pen drive holding only today&#39;s files</td><td>Your files, if someone copies them; malware on your drive</td><td>Scan the drive before opening it on your own laptop</td></tr><tr><th scope="row">4</th><td>A one-time link opened on the shop PC</td><td>The downloaded file, until deleted</td><td>You must clear Downloads and the Recycle Bin</td></tr><tr><th scope="row">5</th><td>Your email in a Guest window</td><td>The downloaded file; your password was typed on their keyboard</td><td>Sign out, then remove the device from your account</td></tr><tr><th scope="row">6</th><td>The shopkeeper&#39;s WhatsApp</td><td>Chat, phone storage, shop PC copy, your phone number</td><td>You can&#39;t check or clean their phone</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<h3 id="printing-straight-from-your-phone">Printing straight from your phone</h3>
<p>Most Android phones come with a print service built on Mopria technology, so the <strong>Print</strong> option in a PDF viewer or Files app can find nearby compatible printers (<a href="https://mopria.org/print-from-android" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Mopria Alliance</a>). The Mopria Print Service app also supports Wi-Fi Direct, a direct phone-to-printer link, if the printer has it switched on. On an iPhone, AirPrint needs your phone and the printer on <a href="https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT201311" rel="noopener" target="_blank">the same Wi-Fi network</a>, so you&#39;d join the shop&#39;s Wi-Fi. Ask at the counter: &quot;Does your printer support Wi-Fi Direct or mobile printing?&quot; If it does, the file never touches their computer.</p>
<h3 id="using-a-pen-drive-safely">Using a pen drive safely</h3>
<p>Keep one cheap pen drive just for printing. Put only the pages you need on it, in PDF, and nothing else. After printing, delete the files from the drive. On your own laptop, keep AutoRun switched off and your antivirus up to date, as <a href="https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/news/using-caution-usb-drives" rel="noopener" target="_blank">CISA recommends</a>, and let the antivirus scan the drive before you open anything on it. Never open an unfamiliar file that appeared on the drive after the shop visit.</p>
<h3 id="if-you-have-to-log-in-on-the-shop-pc">If you have to log in on the shop PC</h3>
<p>Use a Guest window in Chrome or a private window in another browser, sign in, download the file, and close the window when you&#39;re done (<a href="https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/2917834?hl=en" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Google Account Help</a>). Then, on your phone, open your Google Account, go to <strong>Security &amp; sign-in</strong>, then <strong>Your devices</strong>, then <strong>Manage all devices</strong>, and sign out of the shop computer if it appears (<a href="https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3067630?hl=en" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Google Account Help</a>). Typing a password on a stranger&#39;s computer carries its own risks, covered in <a href="/blog/is-it-safe-to-log-in-on-a-cyber-cafe-computer/">whether it&#39;s safe to log in on a cyber cafe computer</a>.</p>
<h2 id="what-to-ask-the-shopkeeper-to-delete-before-you-leave">What to ask the shopkeeper to delete before you leave</h2>
<p>Ask while you&#39;re still at the counter, and watch it happen. A polite &quot;Bhaiya, file delete kar dijiye, Recycle Bin se bhi&quot; works better than hoping they&#39;ll do it later.</p>
<ol data-checklist>
<li>Delete your file from Downloads, the Desktop, and the folder the scanner saves to if anything was scanned.</li>
<li>Empty the Recycle Bin, or delete with Shift+Delete. This won&#39;t defeat recovery tools, but it stops the next customer from finding your file.</li>
<li>Delete the chat and the file from their phone if you sent it on WhatsApp, and from the shop PC if they printed it through WhatsApp Web.</li>
<li>Log out of anything you opened yourself. For WhatsApp Web, that&#39;s <strong>Settings &gt; Log out</strong> on the computer, or <strong>Linked devices</strong>, tap the device, <strong>Log out</strong> on your phone (<a href="https://faq.whatsapp.com/834124628020911/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">WhatsApp Help Center</a>). For email, sign out and close the Guest window.</li>
<li>Collect every misprint and extra copy. Count the pages you paid for and take all of them, including the spoiled ones. Tear them up at home.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="should-you-write-the-purpose-on-id-photocopies">Should you write the purpose on ID photocopies?</h2>
<p>Yes. After the copy is printed, write across it in pen: who it&#39;s for, why, and the date. For example, &quot;For SIM KYC at [store name] only, 12-10-2026.&quot; Sign across the text so part of your signature overlaps the image. Don&#39;t cover the photo or number the receiver needs to read.</p>
<p>Be clear about what this does. It makes it harder to quietly reuse that copy for a loan, a SIM or a rental somewhere else, and it records where the copy went. It does nothing about a clean scan or photo taken before you wrote on it, so don&#39;t leave the original on the copier glass or let anyone photograph your card.</p>
<p>Better still, put less on the page:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Masked Aadhaar.</strong> UIDAI describes it as e-Aadhaar with the first eight digits replaced by x&#39;s and only the last four visible, and it says e-Aadhaar is <a href="https://uidai.gov.in/en/aadhaar-online-services" rel="noopener" target="_blank">just as valid as the physical card</a> under the Aadhaar Act. Where an office accepts it, print that instead of photocopying your card.</li>
<li><strong>DigiLocker.</strong> DigiLocker says documents issued into it carry the <a href="https://www.digilocker.gov.in/web/about/faq" rel="noopener" target="_blank">same legal standing as the paper originals</a>. Where an office accepts DigiLocker, showing it can replace a paper copy entirely.</li>
<li><strong>The Aadhaar app.</strong> As of October 2026, UIDAI lists &quot;Selective Sharing of Aadhaar Verifiable Credential&quot; among the <a href="https://uidai.gov.in/en/faq" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Aadhaar app&#39;s services</a>, which is meant to let you choose which details a verifier sees.</li>
</ul>
<p>In December 2025, UIDAI&#39;s CEO said the authority plans to make hotels and other private verifiers register and move from photocopies to QR and app checks (<a href="https://www.medianama.com/2025/12/223-explained-uidais-new-rule-aadhaar-based-verification/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">MediaNama</a>). Until that reaches every counter, people will keep asking for paper. More on that in our guide to <a href="/blog/how-to-share-aadhaar-copy-safely/">sharing an Aadhaar copy safely</a>.</p>
<h2 id="where-no-sus-fits">Where NO SUS fits</h2>
<p>Two parts of NO SUS help at a print shop, with limits. <a href="/guides/#chat">NO SUS Chat</a> lets you open your files on the shop computer without signing in to Google there. Open your NO SUS domain (like riya.nosus.foo) on that computer, scan its QR code with the NO SUS Android app (there is no iPhone app), match the number, and approve with your phone&#39;s screen lock. The computer shows only your NO SUS Chat, for the time you pick (15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours), and the session ends after 10 minutes without use (an hour on an 8-hour session). If the shop insists on receiving a file, a <a href="/guides/#burn">Burn Files</a> link opens once and then stops working, so your document doesn&#39;t sit in their chat. Either way, downloads and prints can stay on that computer, so do the cleanup above anyway.</p>
