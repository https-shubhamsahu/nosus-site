The short answer

WhatsApp encrypts every personal chat end to end by default and adds optional disappearing messages. Telegram encrypts end to end only in secret chats, which are one-to-one, tied to one device and need to be started by hand; normal Telegram chats are stored encrypted on Telegram's servers. Both let the other person keep a copy, because neither can block a camera, and a WhatsApp backup, forward or quoted reply can outlive its timer.

WhatsApp encrypts every personal chat end to end by default, and its disappearing messages are an optional timer on top. Telegram encrypts end to end only in secret chats, which you start by hand. Neither can stop the person you are talking to from keeping a copy, because a camera still works.

Here is what each one really protects, checked on Telegram's and WhatsApp's own pages on 16 October 2026.

Is Telegram end-to-end encrypted by default?

No. Per Telegram's FAQ, normal private chats and all group chats are cloud chats. They use server-client encryption and sit encrypted in Telegram's cloud, which is why you can open them on any device. Only secret chats use end-to-end encryption, where Telegram says it has no data to disclose.

A secret chat has some strings attached. It is tied to the device where you created it, is not stored in the cloud and is erased when you log out. Messages cannot be forwarded out of it. Deleting a message on your side tells the other app to delete it too.

Can you screenshot a Telegram secret chat?

Yes, on some systems. Telegram says it will try to notify you when a screenshot is taken in a secret chat, but admits there is no bulletproof way to detect screenshots on certain systems, and that someone can always photograph the screen with another device. The self-destruct timer in a secret chat starts when the recipient sees the message and removes it from both devices, which helps against a lost phone, not against a keen recipient.

Telegram added a newer setting in March 2026. Disable Sharing was announced for Telegram Premium subscribers only. It works in private one-to-one chats and, per the post, stops forwarding, screenshots and saving media. Telegram's post does not address a second phone pointed at the screen.

Telegram also has an auto-delete timer for normal chats. As announced, it counts down from sending, not from reading, and removes messages for everyone in the chat. That is not end-to-end encrypted.

Are WhatsApp disappearing messages safe?

The encryption part is solid. WhatsApp's encryption white paper says encryption has no off switch, and defines it as content that even WhatsApp and Meta cannot access. It excludes chats with Meta's own services and with businesses that use Cloud API, so a chat with a company may not be end-to-end encrypted.

The delete part is a tidy-up, not a lock. From WhatsApp's help page:

  • Timers: 24 hours, 7 days or 90 days, affecting only new messages.
  • Previews: a notification preview may stay until the other person opens the app.
  • Forwards: a message forwarded into a chat without disappearing messages will not disappear there.
  • Quotes: a reply may still show the quoted message after it expires.
  • Backups: a backup made before the message disappears includes it, though restoring from that backup deletes it.
  • Kept messages: anyone in a group can keep a message. The sender gets a notice and can unkeep it.
  • Screenshots: the page describes no block or alert for disappearing messages.

What does WhatsApp view once add?

View once is the stricter mode, and the one WhatsApp mode that blocks screenshots. Per WhatsApp, the recipient can't screenshot, forward, copy or save it, and it won't land in their gallery. WhatsApp still warns that someone can film the screen, and that audio can be recorded with a screen recorder.

Its limits matter for documents. View once is for photos, videos and voice messages only. If you need the same behaviour for a document, see how to send a PDF as view once. WhatsApp's separate Advanced Chat Privacy setting blocks exporting chats, auto-saving media and use of messages in AI features, and its post doesn't mention screenshots.

So which one should you use?

  • Chatting with a friend, and want it tidy: WhatsApp with a timer. It is the one with no setup, groups and your normal contacts.
  • A sensitive one-to-one chat you want hidden from the company too: a Telegram secret chat, if you accept one device and no groups. A WhatsApp personal chat is also end-to-end encrypted, so the gap is smaller than it looks.
  • A photo that must not be saved on their phone: WhatsApp view once, or Telegram Disable Sharing if you have Premium.
  • A PDF, ID copy or file: don't rely on a chat timer. A copy forwarded or saved before the timer fires is yours to lose.

Questions people ask

Is Telegram secret chat safe?

Safe from Telegram and anyone in between: only the two devices can read the messages, and Telegram says it has no data to disclose for them. It is not safe from the other person, who can photograph the screen. Telegram admits screenshots cannot be reliably detected on some systems.

Does Telegram secret chat notify screenshots?

Telegram says it will try to alert you when someone screenshots a secret chat, but there is no bulletproof way to detect screenshots on certain systems. Treat the alert as a hint, not a guarantee.

Are WhatsApp disappearing messages safe?

They are end-to-end encrypted like every personal WhatsApp chat, and they delete from both phones after 24 hours, 7 days or 90 days. They are not safe from the recipient. A forward into a chat without a timer, a quoted reply, a screenshot or a photo of the screen can all keep the message.

Can you screenshot WhatsApp disappearing messages?

WhatsApp's help page on disappearing messages describes no screenshot block or alert. Screenshots are blocked only for view once photos, videos and voice messages, and WhatsApp warns that someone can still photograph the screen.

Is Telegram end-to-end encrypted by default?

No. Telegram's FAQ says cloud chats, which cover normal private chats and all groups, use server-client encryption and are stored encrypted on Telegram's servers. Only secret chats are end-to-end encrypted.

Which is safer, Telegram secret chat or WhatsApp?

For privacy from the company, both are comparable when you use them correctly, but WhatsApp does it without any setup. Telegram secret chats add a read-then-delete timer and block forwarding, at the price of one device and no groups. For protection from the other person, neither is safe.
