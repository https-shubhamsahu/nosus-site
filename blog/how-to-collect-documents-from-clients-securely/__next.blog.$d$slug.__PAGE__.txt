1e:T36a1,<p>Tax season, HR onboarding and every new tenant run on the same request in India: send your PAN, Aadhaar and last six months of bank statements on WhatsApp. It works, and it leaves copies of someone&#39;s identity and finances on phones, laptops and inboxes you don&#39;t control. Below is what India&#39;s data protection law actually asks of you, followed by a collection workflow a small office can set up in an afternoon.</p>
<p>This is a practical guide, not legal advice. If your firm handles large volumes of personal data, get a lawyer to look at your specific situation.</p>
<h2 id="why-collecting-documents-on-whatsapp-or-email-is-a-problem">Why collecting documents on WhatsApp or email is a problem</h2>
<p>The apps themselves aren&#39;t the issue. The trouble is what happens to the files after they arrive.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Copies multiply.</strong> One Aadhaar photo can end up in the client&#39;s chat, your chat, your phone gallery, a WhatsApp Web session on the office computer, your email, the Downloads folder and a printout. You can&#39;t delete what you can&#39;t find. If WhatsApp Web was ever open on a shared machine, <a href="/blog/logout-whatsapp-web-from-another-computer">log it out remotely</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Your personal number becomes the office inbox.</strong> Every client, tenant and candidate gets it, and their documents sit next to your family chats.</li>
<li><strong>One wrong tap is a breach.</strong> The DPDP Act&#39;s definition of a personal data breach includes accidental disclosure or sharing of personal data (<a href="https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf" rel="noopener" target="_blank">section 2(u)</a>). Forwarding a client&#39;s bank statement to the wrong chat fits.</li>
<li><strong>There is no record.</strong> You can&#39;t tell who opened a file, and you can&#39;t show that you deleted it.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="does-the-dpdp-act-apply-to-a-ca-landlord-or-small-business">Does the DPDP Act apply to a CA, landlord or small business?</h2>
<p>Very likely, if you collect personal data for work. The <a href="https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023</a> calls anyone who decides why and how personal data is processed a Data Fiduciary, and its definition of a person covers individuals, firms and companies. The Act excludes data processed for any &quot;personal or domestic purpose&quot; (section 3(c)), which is hard to stretch to client files collected for paid work. It also covers paper documents that you later scan or photograph (section 3(a)).</p>
<h3 id="when-the-duties-start">When the duties start</h3>
<p>The <a href="https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf" rel="noopener" target="_blank">DPDP Rules, 2025</a> were published on 13 November 2025 with an <a href="https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190014" rel="noopener" target="_blank">18-month phased timeline</a>. Under Rule 1:</p>
<ul>
<li>Rules setting up the Data Protection Board applied immediately.</li>
<li>Rule 4, on Consent Managers, applies one year after publication, in November 2026.</li>
<li>Rules 3, 5 to 16, 22 and 23, which cover notice, security safeguards, breach reporting and erasure, apply 18 months after publication: mid-May 2027.</li>
</ul>
<p>The Data Fiduciary duties in the Act itself mostly follow the same 18-month date (<a href="https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/india/privacy/meity-notifies-dpdp-rules-enforcement-timelines-and-formation-of-the-board" rel="noopener" target="_blank">The Legal 500</a>). In January 2026, <a href="https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/meity-may-cut-compliance-timeline-for-key-dpdp-rules-to-12-months-126012201293_1.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Business Standard reported</a> that MeitY had proposed a 12-month window for significant data fiduciaries, the bigger players the government designates, and an earlier start for the one-year log retention rule that applies to everyone. Those were proposals, so check <a href="https://www.meity.gov.in/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">meity.gov.in</a> for amended Rules before planning around May 2027.</p>
<h3 id="what-the-law-asks-of-you">What the law asks of you</h3>
<p>These are the duties that matter most for document collection once they apply:</p>
<div class="table-scroll" role="region" aria-label="Table" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Duty</th><th scope="col">Where it comes from</th><th scope="col">What it means in practice</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><th scope="row">Collect only what you need</th><td>Section 6(1)</td><td>Consent covers only the data necessary for the stated purpose.</td></tr><tr><th scope="row">Tell people what and why</th><td>Section 5, Rule 3</td><td>When you ask for consent: an itemised list of the data, the purpose, and how to withdraw consent, use their rights or complain to the Board.</td></tr><tr><th scope="row">Reasonable security safeguards</th><td>Section 8(5), Rule 6</td><td>Encryption or masking, access control, access logs and monitoring, backups, and security terms in contracts with vendors who process data for you.</td></tr><tr><th scope="row">Report breaches</th><td>Section 8(6), Rule 7</td><td>Tell each affected person without delay; give the Board a detailed report within 72 hours.</td></tr><tr><th scope="row">Delete when done</th><td>Section 8(7), Rule 8</td><td>Erase data once the purpose is served or consent is withdrawn, unless a law requires you to keep it.</td></tr><tr><th scope="row">Name a contact</th><td>Section 8(9), Rule 9</td><td>Publish who answers questions about how you handle personal data.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<p>Two details are easy to miss. First, Rule 8(3) requires you to keep personal data and processing logs for at least one year from processing, for specified government purposes, before erasing them. Deleting everything the moment a return is filed will not be enough. Second, not every collection runs on consent. Data a person voluntarily gives you for a purpose they asked for can count as a legitimate use under section 7(a), and so can an employer&#39;s processing for employment under section 7(i); neither needs consent. The security, breach and erasure duties apply either way.</p>
<p>The <a href="https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Act&#39;s penalty schedule</a> sets ceilings of up to ₹250 crore for failing to take reasonable security safeguards and ₹200 crore for failing to report a breach. Those are maximums: the Board has to weigh the gravity of the breach and the type of data involved (section 33(2)). Requests from people to access, correct or erase their data must be answered within 90 days (<a href="https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190014" rel="noopener" target="_blank">PIB</a>).</p>
<h2 id="a-secure-document-collection-workflow-in-7-steps">A secure document collection workflow in 7 steps</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Write a minimum-documents list for each service.</strong> For every job, list exactly what you need and why. Does payroll need a full bank statement, or just the account number and IFSC? Does a rent agreement need the full Aadhaar number, or would a <a href="/blog/how-to-share-aadhaar-copy-safely">masked Aadhaar copy</a> do? If you need a statement, ask for the specific period only.</li>
<li><strong>Give clients one upload link, not your personal number.</strong> Pick one channel from the comparison below and use it for every client. Put it in your email signature, on your website and on your visiting card.</li>
<li><strong>Send a short notice with the link.</strong> For example: <em>For your ITR we need your PAN, Form 16 and bank interest certificates. Please upload them at [link], not on WhatsApp. We use them only for this filing, keep them until [date], then delete them. Questions or deletion requests: [name, email].</em> That gives the itemised list, purpose and contact person. If you rely on consent, then once Rule 3 applies the notice must also say how to withdraw consent, use their rights and complain to the Data Protection Board.</li>
<li><strong>Store everything in one place with access control.</strong> One folder per client, in one account, with two-step verification turned on. Give access only to the people working on that file, and remove it the day someone leaves. Prefer storage that shows an activity or access history, because Rule 6 expects you to be able to detect unauthorised access.</li>
<li><strong>Clean up stray copies the same day.</strong> Once a file is in the client folder, delete it from WhatsApp chats, your phone gallery, the Downloads folder and email. Shred printouts you no longer need. When you send documents back, use an expiring link and send any PDF password through a different channel (<a href="/blog/how-to-send-a-password-securely">how to send a password securely</a>).</li>
<li><strong>Put deletion on the calendar.</strong> For each document type, decide how long you keep it, allowing for any record-keeping law or professional rule that applies to you and the one-year minimum in Rule 8(3). Set a recurring reminder, delete, and note the date you did it.</li>
<li><strong>Write a one-page breach plan.</strong> Decide now what happens if a file goes to the wrong person: ask the recipient to delete it, write down what happened and when, and tell the client. Once Rule 7 applies, you also have to inform the Data Protection Board, with a detailed report within 72 hours.</li>
</ol>

<h3 id="where-no-sus-fits">Where NO SUS fits</h3>
<p>If you want clients to send files without an account and without your number, <a href="/guides#drop">Drop</a> gives you an address like riya-notes.nosus.foo that they open in a browser. The door starts closed: you open it for a set time, optionally with a code, then preview each file and accept it into Google Drive or decline it. Limits: one file of up to 25 MB per send, files you don&#39;t accept are deleted after 24 hours, and after you accept, the file&#39;s safety depends on your Drive settings. More in <a href="/blog/receive-files-without-sharing-your-phone-number">receiving files without sharing your number</a>.</p>
<h2 id="notes-for-cas-hr-teams-landlords-and-coaching-institutes">Notes for CAs, HR teams, landlords and coaching institutes</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>CAs and tax preparers.</strong> Files scatter during the filing rush, so set up the upload link and notice template before it starts. Keep client portal passwords in a password manager, never in a chat.</li>
<li><strong>HR teams.</strong> Collect onboarding documents through your HR system or the shared upload link, not a recruiter&#39;s personal WhatsApp. When a candidate isn&#39;t hired, the purpose is over: schedule their documents for deletion, allowing for any retention a law requires, including Rule 8(3) once it applies.</li>
<li><strong>Landlords and PG owners.</strong> Keep only the ID copies your agreement and any local police verification require, in one folder. Don&#39;t forward tenant IDs to society or building WhatsApp groups.</li>
<li><strong>Coaching institutes.</strong> Students under 18 are children under the Act, and once section 9 applies, you need verifiable parental consent before processing a child&#39;s data. The Rules&#39; exemption for educational institutions (Fourth Schedule) covers only tracking and behavioural monitoring for education or safety, not admission paperwork, so collect admission documents from parents through your upload link.</li>
<li><strong>Freelancers.</strong> The same seven steps work at a smaller scale: one upload link, one folder, one deletion reminder.</li>
</ul>
