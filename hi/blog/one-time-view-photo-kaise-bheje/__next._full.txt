<h2 id="whatsapp-par-one-time-view-photo-kaise-bheje">WhatsApp par one time view photo kaise bheje?</h2>

<ol>
<li>Chat ya group chat kholein.</li>
<li>Attach (paperclip) icon par tap karein. Phir <strong>Camera</strong> se nayi photo ya video lein, ya <strong>Gallery</strong> se phone mein padi photo ya video chunein.</li>
<li>&#39;1&#39; wale icon par tap karein. Icon hara ho jaaye, to View once mode on hai.</li>
<li>Send par tap karein.</li>
</ol>
<p>Har baar View once alag se chunna padta hai, yeh apne aap on nahi rehta. Bhejne ke baad aap khud bhi woh photo dobara nahi dekh sakte. Agar paane wale ne &#39;पढ़े गए मैसेज&#39; (read receipts) on rakha hai, to photo khulne par chat mein &#39;खोला गया&#39; dikhega.</p>
<p>Paane wale ko photo bheje jaane ke 14 din ke andar kholni hoti hai, warna chat se uski samay-seema khatam ho jaati hai (<a href="https://faq.whatsapp.com/1077018839582332/?locale=hi_IN" rel="noopener" target="_blank">WhatsApp</a>). Voice message bhi View once mein jaa sakta hai: mic par tap karke upar swipe karein (recording lock), phir &#39;1&#39; icon aur Send.</p>
