About NO SUS

NO SUS keeps your files on every screen and lets you choose how you send them.

What it does

The NO SUS app has two tabs. Chat is your own chat, stored in your Google Drive, and it opens on any screen from your domain. Send shares a file or a note one of three ways: opened once, watermarked with each viewer’s email, or as traceable copies.

Self-destructing notes and files also work right here on nosus.foo, with no account. The web app is at app.nosus.foo; the only native app is Android. The product guide shows each tool, and Security says what each one protects and what it can’t.

Who runs it

NO SUS is built and run by its founder, Shubham Sahu, in Mumbai, India. He also writes the blog.

Ads and analytics

The app and the note and file tools show no ads; the blog shows Google ads. The website and the web app use Google Analytics to count visits. Google is never sent a note, a file or a link’s key. The privacy policy says what each one collects.
