One small ask: 14 days

Open the app once a day for 14 days. Google Play needs testers to use an app for 14 days in a row before it can be published. Opening it is enough.

How to join in three steps

Use the same Google account as the Play Store on your phone, for every step.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Join the testers group

    Joining makes you eligible straight away.

    Join the group (opens in a new tab)
  2. 2

    Step 2: Become a tester

    Open the Play testing page and tap Become a tester.

    Open the testing page (opens in a new tab)
  3. 3

    Step 3: Install from Google Play

    Then sign in with Google. If it says not available yet, wait 15–30 minutes and try again.

    Get it on Google Play (opens in a new tab)

Then, for 14 days

  • Open the app once a day.
  • Stay in the group until the 14 days are over. Leaving resets the count.
  • Found a bug? Write to us.
