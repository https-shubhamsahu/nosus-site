Watermark a PDF with each viewer's email

NO SUS (nosus.foo) watermarked links share a PDF or image through a link that asks each viewer for their email, then shows that email as a watermark across the document. You get a log of every view and can revoke the link. Viewers need no account. The email is typed, not verified, and documents aren't end-to-end encrypted.

Open the web app
Android beta

How it works in four steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Pick the PDF

    In the NO SUS app (Android, or app.nosus.foo in any browser), open Send and pick the PDF.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Turn on Watermarked

    Choose Anyone with a link, turn on Watermarked and create the link.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Send it

    The viewer types their email and reads the PDF in a watermarked viewer with no download button.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Watch the log, then revoke

    Each link's log shows the email entered, the device, when it was opened and for how long; revoke it when you're done.

Honest limits

  • Watermarked documents are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • The watermark shows the email the viewer typed: an attribution clue, not proof of who they are.
  • A browser can't block screenshots or a phone camera; the watermark and the view log deter and trace, they don't block.
  • Revoking stops new access; anything already captured can't be recalled.

Questions

Does the viewer need an account to open a watermarked PDF?

No. They open the link in any browser and type their email. That email becomes the watermark and appears in your view log. Watermarking is free to use today; you need a NO SUS account to send.

Can I see who opened my PDF?

Yes. Each link's log shows the email entered, whether it was opened in a browser or the app, when, and how long it was viewed. If someone you didn't send it to opens it, that open is logged too, with whatever email they typed.

Can the viewer download or screenshot the PDF?

There is no download button and text selection is off, but a browser can't block screenshots or a phone camera. If a capture leaks, the watermark shows which email it was opened with.

What happens when I revoke the link?

New opens through that link are refused. Anything a viewer already saw, captured or photographed can't be recalled. Links also stop working after 30 days by default.

Can someone remove the watermark from a PDF?

There is no download button, so there is no PDF file to strip a mark from. A screenshot or photo still carries the watermark, though someone could crop or edit it. Treat it as a clue, not proof.

Why watermark a document at all?

A watermark does not stop a leak. It gives you a clue about whose copy leaked. IBM puts the average data breach in India at ₹25.5 crore in 2026, up from ₹22 crore in 2025, so for sensitive files a clue can be worth having.
