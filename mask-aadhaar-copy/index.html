Why a solid box, not blur

Blurred or pixelated digits can sometimes be guessed, since there are only ten of them. A solid box replaces those pixels in your copy. The saved file is a new image drawn by your browser, so it carries none of the photo's hidden details, like location or camera (EXIF).

Your image stays on this device

The page opens, covers, stamps and saves the image inside your browser. The image is never sent anywhere: there is no upload, and our visit counter never sees the file.

Check it yourself: once the page has loaded, turn off your internet. Everything still works.

What it can't do

  • A watermark discourages reuse. It can't stop anyone from photographing, cropping or editing the copy.
  • Masking hides pixels in this copy only. Your card and any copy you gave before are unchanged.
  • Your name, photo, date of birth and address stay visible unless you cover them.

Questions

Is it safe to blur an Aadhaar card photo?

A solid box is safer than blur, because blurred digits can sometimes be guessed. This tool only uses solid boxes.

Which Aadhaar digits should I hide?

The first 8. UIDAI's masked Aadhaar shows only the last 4 digits of the number.

How do I put a watermark on an Aadhaar card copy?

Add the image, type what it's for, who it's for and the date, then download. The line repeats diagonally across the whole image.

Does a watermark stop misuse?

No. It shows what the copy was given for, which makes reuse harder to pass off. Someone can still photograph, crop or edit it.
