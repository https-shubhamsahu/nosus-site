NO SUS — DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open NO SUS and sign in.
  2. Tap your avatar, then Settings.
  3. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete account.
  4. Type your email to confirm.

This permanently and immediately deletes:

  • your profile, profile picture and sign-in record;
  • your handle, your drops and the watermarked links you made;
  • your group memberships and private notes;
  • every file you uploaded, together with its stored copy, including files in groups other people still use;
  • the group activity records of what you did (opening, uploading or deleting files, joining or leaving groups);
  • your device push tokens and notifications;
  • the receipts that tell you whether a link you sent was opened.

A group where you were the only member is deleted with everything in it. If you were a group's only admin, the member who joined earliest becomes admin. This cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email shubhamsahu@nosus.foo from the address on your account with the subject "NO SUS account deletion request". Deletion is completed within 30 days.

What is retained

Your own chat stays in your Google Drive; NO SUS only disconnects from it, so delete it in Drive if you want it gone. Product analytics events (see the privacy policy) and feedback you submitted are kept with your account ID removed, so they no longer link to you. Activity records that other members created stay in their groups; where one pointed at a file you uploaded, the link to that file is removed.
