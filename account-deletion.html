NO SUS — DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open NO SUS and sign in.
  2. Tap your avatar → Profile & Settings.
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone → Delete User Account.
  4. Type your email to confirm.

This permanently deletes your profile, group memberships, uploaded files and their storage objects, your avatar, and your authentication record. Groups where you were the only admin are removed or ownership is transferred. This cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email shubhamsahu9372580326@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "NO SUS account deletion request". Deletion is completed within 30 days.

What is retained

Tamper-evident group audit entries reference actor IDs; after deletion these no longer resolve to any identity. Feedback you submitted is kept in anonymized form (the link to your account is severed). App usage events (see the privacy policy) are kept with your account ID removed, so they are no longer linked to your account.
