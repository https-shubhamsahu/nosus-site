Receive a file from someone who doesn’t have the app

Ask the sender to open nosus.foo, choose File in the Burn tool and add up to 10 files, 50 MB in all. The files are encrypted in their browser, and they send you the link by email or any chat. Neither of you needs an account, and the link opens once. NO SUS does not give you an upload page for strangers.

Tap File · Encrypted in your browser

0/50,000

While its 2-digit code is valid (20 min), the key is also held on our server.

How it works in four steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Send them to nosus.foo

    Ask the sender to open nosus.foo in any browser, on a phone or a computer.

  2. 2

    Step 2: They add the files

    They tap File in the Burn tool and add up to 10 files, 50 MB combined.

  3. 3

    Step 3: They send you the link

    They copy the one-time link and send it to you however they reach you, like email.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Open it once

    You open the link, save what you need, and the files are deleted from our server.

Honest limits

  • The sender still has to reach you somehow to pass on the link, such as email.
  • Each link opens once, so save the files when you open it.
  • While a single file’s 2-digit code is valid (20 minutes), its key is held on our server too.
  • Nothing checks who the sender really is.

Questions

Does Google Drive have a file request link?

Not as such. Google’s nearest option is a Google Form with a file upload question, and every sender must sign in to a Google account.

Does the sender need an account or the app?

No. They make the link at nosus.foo in any browser. You don’t need an account to open it either.

Can people upload files to my NO SUS domain?

No. Your domain opens your own NO SUS Chat on another screen after you approve it on your phone. It does not take uploads from other people.

Can I collect documents from many clients this way?

Only one link at a time, from each sender. For one link that many people upload to, a Google Form or a Dropbox file request fits better.

What happens to the file after I open it?

It is deleted from our server after one open, or when the link expires if nobody opens it.
