Print at a xerox shop without logging in to Gmail or WhatsApp

Open your NO SUS domain, like riya.nosus.foo, on the shop’s computer. Scan the QR it shows with your phone, match the number and approve. Your NO SUS Chat opens there, and the file is ready to print. You never type your Gmail password there or send the file to the shop’s WhatsApp.

How it works in five steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Put the file in your chat

    Before you go, add the file to NO SUS Chat on your phone. It is stored in your own Google Drive.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Open your domain

    At the counter, ask for your domain to be opened in the shop computer’s browser. It shows a QR code.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Scan and match

    Scan the QR with the NO SUS app, then tap the number that matches the one on the shop’s screen.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Approve

    Approve with your phone’s screen lock. Your chat opens on the shop computer; open the file there.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Print, then clean up

    Print it, delete it from the computer’s Downloads and close the tab. The session ends within an hour anyway.

Honest limits

  • Downloads and printed copies may stay on the shop computer, so delete the file before you leave.
  • You need the NO SUS app on your phone, with the file already in your chat.
  • The shop computer sees what you open on it, so only approve a number shown on the screen in front of you.

Questions

Is it safe to log in to Gmail at a xerox shop?

It’s risky. A shared computer can keep you signed in or save what you type. If you must, use a guest or private window and check your signed-in devices from your phone afterwards. NO SUS Chat avoids typing your password there at all.

Do I need to install anything on the shop computer?

No. You only open your domain in its browser. The NO SUS app runs on your phone.

Does the shop computer get my Google password?

No. The computer never receives your Google password or token. It shows your chat for the session you approve, up to an hour.

Will my file stay on the shop’s computer?

It can. Downloads and prints may remain on that computer after the session ends, so delete the file from Downloads before you leave.

What do I need before I go?

The NO SUS app on your phone, signed in with Google, with the file already in your NO SUS Chat. The chat is stored in your own Drive.
