A loan in your name that you never took? Do this.

  • Someone used your Aadhaar or PAN to take a loan, buy a SIM or open an account.
  • It shows up as recovery calls, or as a line on your credit report you do not know.
  • It is not yours until you dispute it in writing. The steps are below.
  • Free steps
  • No account
  • Letters in English and Hindi

Free steps, in this order

  1. 1

    Step 1: Report it

    Call 1930 if money was taken from you. File on cybercrime.gov.in and write down the acknowledgement number. Ask your local police for an FIR too.1: National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (opens in a new tab)

    cybercrime.gov.in (opens in a new tab)

  2. 2

    Step 2: Check how many SIMs are on your Aadhaar

    Open Sanchar Saathi, choose Know Your Mobile Connections (TAFCOP), and enter your number and OTP. It lists every mobile connection in your name. Mark each number that is not yours as "This is not my number" and press Report. That is how you block a SIM on your Aadhaar that you never took: the operator re-verifies it and disconnects it within 60 days if it fails.2: Sanchar Saathi: Know your mobile connections (TAFCOP) (opens in a new tab)

    tafcop.sancharsaathi.gov.in (opens in a new tab)

  3. 3

    Step 3: Check loans on your PAN with your free credit reports

    To check if someone took a loan on your PAN card, read your credit reports. RBI makes each bureau give you one full report free every calendar year. There are four: CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF High Mark. Look for loans and enquiries you do not know: each one is a possible fake loan on your PAN, and you dispute it in step 5. Check your current mobile and email are on file, so the bureau and lender alerts (RBI circular 2023-24/73) can reach you.3: RBI circular 2023-24/73: customer service by credit bureaus and lenders (alerts, free yearly report) (opens in a new tab)

    cibil.com (opens in a new tab)experian.in (opens in a new tab)equifax.co.in (opens in a new tab)crifhighmark.com (opens in a new tab)

  4. 4

    Step 4: Check and lock your Aadhaar

    On myAadhaar or in the mAadhaar app, open Authentication History to see who verified you in the last six months. Then turn on Lock Biometrics. Fingerprint, iris and face checks stop. OTP checks still work.4: UIDAI: Aadhaar authentication history and biometric lock FAQs (opens in a new tab)

    myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in (opens in a new tab)

  5. 5

    Step 5: Write to the lender, then the bureaus

    Tell the lender in writing that the loan is not yours and ask for its KYC record. Send a dispute to each bureau. Wait 30 days. If there is no reply, or the reply does not fix it, complain to the RBI Integrated Ombudsman. Under the scheme in force since 1 July 2026, you have 90 days to file, counted from the end of that 30-day wait or from their last reply, whichever is later.5: Equifax India: customer grievance redressal (opens in a new tab)6: Experian India: how to raise a dispute (opens in a new tab)7: CRIF High Mark: how to raise a dispute (opens in a new tab)8: RBI: Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2026 FAQs (in force from 1 July 2026; time limits in Q17) (opens in a new tab)

    cms.rbi.org.in (opens in a new tab)

What it cannot do

  • A letter does not remove an entry. The lender and the bureau do. The letters ask them to, in writing, and start the clock.
  • It does not find who did it.
  • Some lenders take longer than 30 days.
  • Rules and portals change. Check the links before you file.

Questions

A loan is in my name that I never took. What do I do first?

Report it: call 1930 if money was taken, and file on cybercrime.gov.in. Then check your credit reports and the SIMs on your Aadhaar, and write to the lender saying the loan is not yours.

How do I check loans taken on my PAN card for free?

Get your credit report from all four bureaus: CIBIL, Experian, Equifax and CRIF High Mark. RBI makes each one give you a full report free every calendar year. Any loan or enquiry you do not know could be a fake loan on your PAN: dispute it with the bureau, write to the lender, and file on cybercrime.gov.in.

How do I remove a fraud loan from my CIBIL report?

Raise a dispute with CIBIL and mark the account as not yours. CIBIL asks the lender to verify it. Write to the lender yourself too, with your police complaint. If the account is still there after 30 days, you can complain to the RBI Integrated Ombudsman.

How many SIM cards are on my Aadhaar, and how do I block one?

Open Sanchar Saathi, choose Know Your Mobile Connections (TAFCOP), and enter your mobile number and OTP. It shows every mobile connection in your name. Mark a number you never took as "This is not my number" and press Report: the operator re-verifies it and disconnects it within 60 days if it fails. One person can hold at most 9 connections across all operators (6 in J&K, Assam and the North East).

The lender will not reply. Where do I complain?

If the lender has not replied in 30 days, or its reply does not fix the problem, complain to the RBI Integrated Ombudsman on cms.rbi.org.in. File within 90 days of the end of that 30-day wait, or of the lender's last reply, whichever is later. Keep your first complaint and any reply.
