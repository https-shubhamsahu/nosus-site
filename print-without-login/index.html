How to print a document at a xerox shop without logging in

Open your NO SUS domain, like riya.nosus.foo, on the shop’s computer. Scan its QR with the NO SUS app on your phone, match the number and approve. Your NO SUS Chat opens there; open the file and print it. You never type your Gmail password on their PC or send the file to the shop’s WhatsApp.

Open the web app
Type your domain at nosus.foo/goAndroid app

How it works in five steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Put the file in your chat

    Before you go, add the file to NO SUS Chat on your phone. It is stored in your own Google Drive.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Open your domain

    At the counter, ask for your domain to be opened in the shop computer’s browser. It shows a QR code.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Scan and match

    Scan the QR with the NO SUS app, then tap the number that matches the one on the shop’s screen.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Approve

    Approve with your phone’s screen lock. Your chat opens on the shop computer; open the file there.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Print, then clean up

    Print it, delete it from the computer’s Downloads and close the tab. If you forget, the session ends on its own after 10 minutes without use (an hour on an 8-hour session).

Honest limits

  • Downloads and printed copies may stay on the shop computer, so delete the file before you leave.
  • You need the NO SUS app on your phone, with the file already in your chat.
  • The shop computer sees what you open on it, so only approve a number shown on the screen in front of you.

Questions

How do I print a document at a xerox shop without logging in?

Put the file in NO SUS Chat on your phone, open your NO SUS domain on the shop’s computer, scan its QR with the NO SUS app, match the number and approve. Your chat opens there; open the file and print it. Your Gmail password is never typed on their PC.

How do I print from my phone without installing an app?

Open nosus.foo in your phone’s browser, add the file and tap Use a code instead, then enter that 6-digit code at nosus.foo on the shop’s computer. The file opens once and the code lasts up to 10 minutes, during which its key is also held on our server. Sending it to the shop’s WhatsApp or email leaves a copy with them.

Is it safe to print a PDF at a cyber cafe?

Mostly, if you don’t log in to your main accounts there and you clean up afterwards. Print a masked or watermarked version where you can, delete the file from Downloads and the Recycle Bin, and close the browser window before you leave.

How do I delete my files from a cyber cafe computer?

Delete the file from the Downloads folder, empty the Recycle Bin, then close every Guest or private window. Google says files you download stay on the computer even in Incognito until you delete them. If the file went to the shop’s WhatsApp or email, ask them to delete it while you watch.

Is it safe to log in to Gmail at a xerox shop?

It’s risky. A shared computer can keep you signed in or save what you type. If you must, use a guest or private window and check your signed-in devices from your phone afterwards. NO SUS Chat avoids typing your password there at all.

How do I print my Aadhaar at a xerox shop safely?

Download a masked e-Aadhaar first. UIDAI says it hides the first 8 digits and shows only the last 4, and that e-Aadhaar is as valid as the physical copy. Put the PDF in your NO SUS Chat and print that.
