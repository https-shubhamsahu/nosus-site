Report a problem

Tell us about a NO SUS link, file, note or account that is being used to harm someone. You do not need an account.

This page sends nothing by itself. It opens an email to our Grievance Officer with your answers filled in, and you press send.

If someone is in danger, call 112. For money lost to cyber fraud, call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in.

What is it about?

A link, a handle like name.nosus.foo, or where it was sent to you. If you paste a NO SUS link, the key after ?k= is taken out for you.

So we can tell you what we did. Leave it empty and we still act on the report.

No email app? Write to shubhamsahu@nosus.foo with the same details.

What happens next

  • We acknowledge your report within 24 hours and resolve it within 7 days.
  • Intimate images and anything involving a child are handled first.
  • We cannot read one-open notes and files: they are encrypted with a key we never get. With the link, we can delete a note or file that has not been opened yet, turn off a watermarked link, and ban the account behind it when there is one.
