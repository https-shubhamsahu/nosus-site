FAQ

Straight answers.

Read the product guide

Do I need an account to try a burn note?

No. Burn Notes and Burn Files need no account for the sender or the recipient, so you can make one right here on nosus.foo.

How do Burn Notes and Burn Files work?

They are encrypted in your browser with AES-256 before upload, open once, and are then deleted from our server. While a burn's 2-digit pairing code is valid (20 minutes by default), its key is held on our server too.

What is my domain?

Your own address on NO SUS, like riya.nosus.foo. Open it on any screen and it shows a QR. Scan it with the NO SUS app, match the number and approve, and your NO SUS Chat opens there.

What can a borrowed computer see?

Only what you approve on your phone, for that session. It never gets your Google password or token, and your files stay in your own Google Drive. Anything you download or print there can stay on that computer.

Can NO SUS block screenshots in Chrome?

No. No website can block screenshots in Chrome. NO SUS stamps the viewer's email on the page and logs who opened it, so a leak can be traced. In the Android app, screenshots are blocked.

What happens if your servers get hacked?

While a burn's 2-digit code is still valid, a break-in could reveal its key; once the code is used or expires, the key is swept shortly after, leaving only encrypted data. Other stored documents aren't end-to-end encrypted, so a full storage breach could expose them.

Is there an iPhone or Android app?

Android is in closed beta on Google Play. On iPhone or any computer, use the web app at app.nosus.foo.
